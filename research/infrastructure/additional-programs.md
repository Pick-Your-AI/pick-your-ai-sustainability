## Concrete public-benefit use and valuation method

A suitable **unexecuted, bounded receiving proposal** is one public static evidence/preview corpus, selected at an immutable source revision, with a manifest of file hashes, total bytes, required retention and expected requests. Its purpose would be reliable public access to explanatory material and reproducible evidence without sending contributor identities, credentials, raw private logs or unlicensed model data to a new provider. No corpus, provider account, deployment or traffic test is created by this research.

Before selecting a candidate, the existing owner would record the uncovered N01/N02/N03 requirement and reuse existing build/CI receipts. Compare that one corpus and a reversible preview route against the current destination: route correctness, redirects/security headers, accessibility, cache behavior, origin/egress charges, export/restore and a documented rollback to the current origin. A CDN is not the independent database backup. Native device checks and calibrated model studies remain with their existing owners.

Use actual quantities and applicable terms to estimate **net avoided cost**: eligible uncovered usage at the current comparable rate, less uncovered services, migration labor, recurring operations, taxes and exit cost. Record currency, billing period and expiration; restrict usable credits to admitted workloads completed within the award term. Do not add headline credits to cash, convert already-free public CI into savings, count the same hosting need twice, or treat a paid-plan comparison as current spending. Actual usage, rates applicable to an applicant, contributor availability and implementation effort are Unknown, so monetary value and hours remain null.

P2 is a qualitative research priority because conditional services could support the public artifact/website need. The deferred historical DigitalOcean route is P3. The supplied 20/20/15/10/10/10/10/5 rubric is retained without manufactured scores: eligibility, net value, likelihood, timing, effort and independence evidence do not support eight numeric component values or a total.

Any later agreement must preserve independent evaluation, publication of valid findings and the right to leave. Separate factual acknowledgments/customer listings from endorsements or influence over rankings. Review actual confidentiality, logo, reporting and publication terms; the research does not assert an unwritten right or provider veto. Preserve source, configuration, portable public artifacts and separate backups; plan DNS/cache rollback, exports before expiry, credential removal, retained invoices and lawful deletion. No service migration or public endorsement is approved by this memo.
