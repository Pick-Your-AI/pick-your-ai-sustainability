## Obligations, renewal and source conflicts

Under OSS v5, use is limited to noncommercial development meeting the Open Source Definition. Seats are assigned per user, not concurrently shared; products are nontransferable. Team Tools are outside the Toolbox product definition. Renewal requires a reply to the renewal email or the renewal form before expiry and remains discretionary under then-current terms. Marketing permission covers the customer's name/trademarks; optional feedback carries broad reuse rights. Account access, applicable privacy/third-party terms and export restrictions also require review before acceptance. These summarized conditions do not replace the full agreement. [J3]

Do not copy the personal subscription's renewal rule into the group OSS plan. There is also a current **personal-route source conflict**: the FAQ describes automatic annual renewal after use within the last six months, while noncommercial agreement v3.0, effective December 16, 2025, §6 describes a written renewal request 30 days before expiry. Preserve both; obtain the actual account/offer terms or provider clarification before relying on renewal. Neither source establishes this project's expiration date. [J5, J7]

No mandatory repository badge, report cadence, exclusivity commitment or special restriction on published research conclusions was established in the reviewed group materials. Missing intake/offer terms remain Unknown; absence of an observed condition is not a guarantee that none exists. Do not offer favorable rankings, private data, redistribution rights or publication control in return for licenses. There is no evidence of a metered OSS-seat overage balance: additional products/users and AI have separate conditions, and no paid expansion is authorized.

### Privacy distinctions that matter to the choice

The July 7, 2026 Product Data Collection Notice distinguishes anonymous telemetry from detailed code-related data, which can include source snippets and full AI interactions and can be used for model training. It describes a one-year retention period for detailed data. Its licensing table says community OSS licenses require consent, personal noncommercial licenses may collect detailed data by default with a settings opt-out, and free IntelliJ/PyCharm Community modes disable detailed collection. These are documented product conditions, not a readback of anyone's installation. [J8]

The personal FAQ's non-optional anonymous statistics must not be confused with the detailed-data setting. Noncommercial agreement §4 separately discusses default detailed collection, its opt-out and compliance monitoring. Treat any broader conflict between introductory consent language and license-specific defaults as unresolved before activation; do not promise a universal opt-out or no collection. [J5, J7, J8]

The Privacy Notice also covers account/service delivery, licensing checks, security and service providers. Actual account settings, installed versions, plugins, individual agreements and data sharing remain Unknown. Keep credentials, private findings and confidential research outside any proposed telemetry/AI workflow unless its actual rights and approved operating scope have been established. This assessment accesses public sources only. [J9]
