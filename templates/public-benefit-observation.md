# Public-benefit observation record

Use with the [measurement plan](../docs/funding/public-benefit-measurement-plan.md). This is a blank **public-safe summary template**, not a participant form or an approved study. Leave unobserved values **Unknown**, not zero. Store any approved private evidence only in its real authorized system; do not add participant rows, consent, linkage, private URLs or identifying hashes here.
