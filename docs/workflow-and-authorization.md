# Workflow and authorization

Owner issue: E09.02. Status: draft; actual organizational roles and acceptance remain unresolved.

## Two independent tracks

Issue-work status describes delivery: Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, Blocked, Done or Not Pursuing. Opportunity lifecycle describes support: Discovered, Verified, Qualified, Preparing, Submitted, Awarded, Declined, Activated or Reporting.

Done means the issue's researched outcome and review are accepted. An ineligible investigation can be Done while the opportunity is Not Pursuing. Preparing or a draft email does not mean submitted. Awarded does not mean received or usable.

Research readiness must be visible separately from an application gate. A researcher may examine a 501(c)(3)-dependent program immediately; the application remains blocked by its verified legal prerequisite.

## Action authority

| Action | Required evidence before acting |
| --- | --- |
| Read public sources and draft findings | In-scope research task and applicable data/source boundaries |
| Publish research/issues | Repository authority, independent review where required, accurate labels and limitations |
| Send an outreach message | Explicit scope-specific permission, verified recipient, reviewed exact text and representations |
| Submit an application | Eligible legal applicant, current terms, complete application, budget/commitments, approved submission scope and authorized submitter |
| Negotiate or accept terms | Actual signatory mandate, conflict/independence review, legal/financial review appropriate to the commitment |
| Open accounts or spend | Explicit financial authority, accepted controls, concrete approved account/spend scope |
| Activate a benefit | Accepted terms and role, usable resource evidence, operating controls and obligations |
| Announce a sponsor or tax treatment | Confirmed status and permitted language; no tax-deductibility claim from project intent alone |

An authorization record identifies the approving person and capacity, exact applicant, program/round, reviewed artifact, requested benefit, permitted recipient/action, obligations, maximum approved commitment and any validity condition. Use [the approval template](../templates/application-approval.md). Unfilled values stay unresolved; they do not authorize action.

Changes to the applicant, requested amount, deliverables, material terms, data handling, exclusivity or deadline require a fresh check of the applicable authority. Never copy a prior approval to another provider.

E01.01 establishes the administrative baseline; E02.06 establishes financial controls; E01.08 addresses neutrality. Completion of this procedure requires their applicable outputs or explicit remaining gates. The existing [organization contribution rules](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) govern GitHub access and review; they do not create banking or legal-signature authority.
