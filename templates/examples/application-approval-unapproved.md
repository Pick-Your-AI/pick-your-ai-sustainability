## SYN-AUTH-02: a material amendment cannot reuse a decision

Extend only this fictional exercise with a hypothetical approval for revision A. It covers one message, recipient and representation, without financial or publication obligation. This is a scenario assumption, not an actual approval, appointment or sent message.

Revision B changes the applicant to a fiscal host and promises the provider exclusive review of benchmark findings before publication. The host relationship and the promise's compatibility with independence requirements are unverified. The hypothetical prior decision covers only A's exact scope.

**Manual result:** retain A and its hypothetical decision; block B's proposed outreach. Record the changed applicant, representation, artifact and material publication/independence restriction. Obtain applicable evidence, mandate and independent review, then a new exact decision before relying on B. Do not overwrite A, assume host status belongs to the project or turn negotiation permission into signing/acceptance permission. Independently valid unchanged scopes keep their result.

In a separate fictional branch, a source recheck confirms identical applicable terms, parties, message, authority and validity conditions. Document continuing validity of the existing scoped approval; a later review date alone does not require duplicate permission. No branch actually sends anything.
