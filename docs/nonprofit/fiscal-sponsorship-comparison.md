## Research recommendation

Compare all three against the same actual needs before preparing an inquiry. The fastest public signup is not evidence of the best legal or operational fit. Host the comparison under this one issue so donation-channel and grant research reuse it.

First collect documentary ownership and authority facts, current licenses and the public record of project maturity. Then request each host’s actual agreement, current fees and service scope only through an authorized representative. Ask how contractor expenses, restricted gifts, account ownership, information disclosure, intellectual-property custody, fundraising receipts and project closure work. A term that is Unknown remains an open question rather than a presumed benefit.

Do not imply that a host’s charitable status transfers to the project. Google for Nonprofits, for example, explicitly excludes US fiscally sponsored organizations without their own 501(c)(3) status under its [current US eligibility rules](https://support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/3215869). Specific providers must confirm the precise applicant/account/benefit route.

## Definition of done

An independent reviewer can trace every material term to its provider’s current source, compare the same service needs and fee basis, identify the unresolved legal and governance questions and distinguish hosting from grants. Research completion creates no affiliation, acceptance, deductible-status claim or authority to transfer assets.
