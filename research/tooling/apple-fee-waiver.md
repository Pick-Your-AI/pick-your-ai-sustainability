### Membership intake and the dated EU conflict

Qualifying nonprofit, educational or government legal entities can request membership relief; individuals, sole proprietors and single-person businesses are excluded. An active Paid Applications Agreement or other digital-goods/services sales through any app conflict with the published conditions. A new organization requests during enrollment; an existing Account Holder requests before expiry. Apple reviews eligibility and may request documents. Prior fees are not refunded, and annual eligibility reaffirmation is required. A1 additionally restricts a waiver holder operating an EU marketplace to distributing apps from developers with the same waiver [A1].

There is no published competition round or fixed closing date in A1. The relevant event is enrollment or the actual membership renewal; that account date and its timezone are **Unknown**, so deadline fields remain null. A4 describes manual renewal beginning 30 days before expiry and also renewal after expiry; that is not evidence of a 30-day waiver grace period. Check the current waiver route before paying or allowing membership to expire [A4]. The public help URL is the recorded intake guidance endpoint, not a verified application form.

**Do not extend the membership finding into universal EU transaction-fee relief.** A1 still describes the former Core Technology Fee and Alternative Terms Addendum. A6 says the August 18 update introduced unified EU terms effective October 1, 2026, with Attachment 14 replacing earlier EU addenda, and says an account moves after accepting the updated agreement; earlier accrued obligations remain. A7 explicitly limits its legacy reporting guidance by that same account-acceptance condition. These are retained as a dated source conflict, not reconciled by guessing which agreement the project signed. No EU transaction-fee savings are included in this recommendation [A1, A6, A7].
