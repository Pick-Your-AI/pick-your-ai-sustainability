# Qualification and scoring

Status: program rubric implementing the supplied master brief.

## Eligibility gate

First evaluate the exact applicant and program. Maintain Current, Fiscal-sponsored and Independent 501(c)(3) route assessments separately. Unknown, Not Verified, Conditionally Eligible, Eligible and Ineligible are distinct findings. A conditional route names its prerequisite and remains unavailable for application until resolved. Future recognition does not automatically satisfy other program requirements.

Only potentially viable opportunities receive an out-of-100 score. The score is an ordering aid after the gate. It cannot turn legal ineligibility into viability.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence expected |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Public benefit and mission fit | 20 | Exact useful deliverable and intended beneficiaries |
| Current or realistically attainable eligibility | 20 | Governing terms and concrete prerequisite path |
| Net usable value | 15 | Likely usage and actual cost otherwise incurred |
| Evidence-backed likelihood of support | 10 | Published selection/entitlement conditions and documented fit; no fabricated award probability |
| Timing and urgency | 10 | Confirmed round/deadline or actual operational need |
| Application and maintenance effort | 10 | Required artifacts, obligations and sustainable capacity |
| Independence, legal and operating risk | 10 | Restrictions, conflicts, exit/overage/privacy exposure |
| Long-term strategic value | 5 | Reusable public capability and sustainable continuation |

Use 0–5 evidence-based ordinal ratings within each criterion, where 0 is the weakest and 5 the strongest result. For effort and risk, a higher rating means lower burden or better-controlled risk. Calculate each contribution as weight × rating / 5. Explain each rating and cite its evidence. This normalization implements the specified weights; it is not a statistical model.

If a factor cannot be defended, store null and leave the total null. A range or qualitative confidence can be shown with its method, but do not invent a point score to fill a Project field. Cash and avoided costs remain separate; headline credit amounts are not usable value.

## Priority

| Priority | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| P0 | Verified urgent deadline or serious operational blocker requiring immediate attention |
| P1 | High-value viable opportunity or foundational work that should begin promptly |
| P2 | Promising opportunity with qualifications, dependencies or additional research |
| P3 | Speculative, distant or low-value opportunity |
| Not Pursuing | Verified ineligible, misaligned, expired without renewal, excessively restrictive or disproportionately costly |

There is no numerical threshold in the master brief mapping a total to P0–P3. Do not invent one. Record the score, gate and priority rationale together. Timing cannot create an urgent deadline without an official source and exact round.

Foundational legal, research and operating tasks receive a priority from the applicable rule, with score marked Not applicable. Their importance is not an invented grant success probability. Review priority whenever eligibility, need, terms, source evidence or a real deadline changes.
