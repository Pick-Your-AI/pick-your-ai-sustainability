## Useful life, accounting and defensible value

**Useful life is an estimate to support with evidence, not a fixed grant value.** For the actual proposed unit, obtain model/manufacture and service history, condition/battery/storage diagnostics where relevant, supported OS/firmware and required toolchain end dates, warranty scope, parts/repair availability, workload suitability, utilization and a return/retirement plan. The planned service period cannot exceed an enforceable loan window and must be reconsidered when support or suitability changes. Warranty length and “less than seven years old when donated” are different observations from remaining productive life. [C2, T6]

No three-year/five-year life, depreciation percentage, residual value or initial carrying amount is assigned here. If the responsible accounting review selects straight-line depreciation for an owned capital asset, an explicit scenario is **(accepted initial asset basis minus supported residual value) / supported service periods**; each input, framework, capitalization threshold, in-service date and method remains Unknown. A borrowed unit must not simply be recorded as an owned donated asset. The actual arrangement could require different treatment for use rights or leases; this research does not decide that legal/accounting classification.

FASB's issued September 2020 ASU 2020-07 explains amendments concerning contributed nonfinancial assets, including equipment and use of assets. It calls for separate presentation and disclosure by category, use/monetization, restrictions and valuation techniques/inputs. The Update expressly is not itself the authoritative Codification and did not change underlying contribution recognition/measurement requirements. It supplies a conditional U.S. nonprofit-accounting reference, not proof that this project uses that framework or that a hypothetical unit is recognized revenue. The actual recipient and its financial reviewer must establish the applicable treatment. [A2]

IRS Publication 561, revised December 2025, addresses donor/appraiser valuation of noncash gifts. Condition, relevant market evidence and obsolescence matter; replacement price alone is not automatically fair market value. That donor-tax guidance is separate from the recipient's accounting basis, this project's avoided cost and the donor's actual allowable deduction. No qualified-recipient assertion, tax receipt, deduction, appraisal or asset valuation is issued here. [A3]

Compare mutually exclusive ways to satisfy the same accepted task: existing compatible equipment, admitted hosted access, borrowing, discounted refurbishment or a gift. **Net incremental usable value = evidenced cost of a necessary alternative actually avoided, less acquisition/administrative fees, shipping/taxes/insurance, setup and supported operation, maintenance, return/sanitization/disposal and other uncovered obligations.** Use dated currency-specific quotations and measured demand; disclose uncertainty and avoid counting both an asset's face value and the same avoided spend. Nothing here establishes that a purchase would otherwise occur, so actual usable value remains null. Record application, setup, operator, maintenance, evidence and exit effort separately when measured; all actual hours remain Unknown.
