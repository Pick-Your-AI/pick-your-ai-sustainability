## Project summary

Pick Your AI is a community initiative developing an accessible builder for configuring AI systems. Its intended experience helps people examine workloads, models, infrastructure, retrieval, agents, training, deployment and operating costs, with clear evidence and unknowns behind compatibility decisions.

The public website is currently a synthetic-data preview. The community maintains sourced research, shared data contracts and developing web/mobile/contributor tools in separate repositories. Current [website](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-website/blob/main/README.md), [research](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/main/README.md), [Apple](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-apple/blob/main/README.md), [Android](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-android/blob/main/README.md) and [leaderboard](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-leaderboard/blob/main/README.md) descriptions were inspected on 2026-10-19 and must be refreshed before submission.
