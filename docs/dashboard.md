## Resources and obligations

Report received cash, committed cash, activated in-kind capacity and realized avoided costs in separate sections and currencies. Application count, award headline values and unused credits are excluded from cash. Unknown balances are not zero.

Report confirmed obligations, submission receipts, benefit expiry, approved renewals, unresolved operators and outstanding restricted-purpose reporting. Due dates come from actual terms, never from a template or roadmap horizon.

## Needs and risks

Show current needs with their receiving technical issues, existing free capabilities, funded/covered scope, remaining gap and valuation confidence. Apply [risk definitions](risk-indicators.md) with denominator and coverage visible. No overall composite score is inferred from activity counts.

Each monthly report records cutoff timestamp, GitHub Project URL, issue IDs, source-register revision, calculation method, independent reviewer, discrepancies, repairs and inaccessible fields. A reviewer must be able to recompute the counts and trace every significant resource claim to evidence.
