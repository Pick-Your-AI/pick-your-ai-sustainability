# Vendor neutrality and sponsorship ethics — draft

Owner issue: E01.08. Status: policy proposal; organizational/legal adoption remains a separate decision.

Sponsorship supports public capability and sustainable operations. It cannot buy favorable compatibility outcomes, rankings, benchmark choices, research conclusions, deletion of adverse findings or exclusion of a competing technology.

## Proposed commitments

Identify sponsor relationships separately from organic comparisons and research evidence. Disclose material constraints, affiliate relationships, contributor conflicts and support affecting a published study. Sponsors may correct factual errors through the same evidence process as other participants; no sponsor receives a veto over supported findings.

Review publication rights, benchmark restrictions, exclusivity, naming, branding, confidentiality, data access and renewal terms before acceptance. Keep a versioned decision record with the reviewed agreement scope. A provider's offer may be rejected if its restrictions defeat the public purpose or feasible independent operation.

Acknowledge actual support accurately: offered, awarded, received, activated and realized are different claims. State cash separately from used in-kind benefits and explain valuation methods. Do not publish private donor information or confidential agreement contents; publish an authorized summary with material conditions.
