## 1. What can be measured now, and what cannot

The intended task is to help someone compare AI-system options for an explicit workload, budget and constraints, understand the evidence and uncertainty, and record why an option is suitable or unsuitable. The measures below test these specific actions; they do not presume that a prototype makes better purchase decisions or that any model is universally best. A useful result can be recognizing that the evidence is insufficient.

These are **inspected source snapshots**, not a claim that each pin is every repository's latest main or a deployed release. Recheck the actual receiving revision before choosing a task or making an external claim.

Every observation must carry one of these labels, plus the exact scope:

- **Synthetic demonstration:** fabricated inputs or an illustrative calculation. It can exercise an instrument or contract; it supplies no real price, participant, model-performance or outcome evidence.
- **Implemented behavior:** source inspection or an actual execution receipt at a pinned revision. Identify which; source review alone is not execution, and execution alone is not a human-benefit study.
- **Validated research within a stated scope:** an actual method, eligible observations, source/conditions and independent findings review support the precise claim. Record vendor/source-only evidence as such until the claimed validation exists.
- **Future work / proposed target:** an unperformed task, unselected protocol or desired result. **Unknown** is the baseline until eligible evidence exists; it is never an implied zero.

## 2. Outcome/evidence matrix

The targets are **proposed internal quality criteria**, authored for review before collection. They are not achieved results, grant requirements, population effect sizes or replacements for Research #63's selected pilot flags. An 80% task criterion is a proposed triage threshold; 100% traceability or zero unresolved critical barriers reflects the importance of not concealing evidence gaps. None has been statistically calibrated. A receiving owner may revise a target prospectively with rationale and a new version, before inspecting the corresponding outcome data.

## 3. Baselines, sampling and interpretation

Use the [observation template](../../templates/public-benefit-observation.md) to declare the population, unit, task, rubric, denominator, exclusions, cutoff and target version **before collection**. Start with a bounded, justified population owned by the receiving work item: for example, all claims in one pinned evidence card, or a prospectively selected critical task. This plan selects no participant sample size or new recruitment route.

Record a baseline using the same task/rubric and measurement conditions intended for follow-up. If comparing an existing alternative and a prototype, the receiving protocol must first choose those conditions, order/counterbalancing and matching rules. A later catalog, pricing, model, dependency graph, task or scoring change may change the question; either justify comparability prospectively or report separate cohorts. There is currently **no accepted baseline/prototype task comparison or measured effect** in this plan.

Use exact integer counts `k/N` only when N is known and positive. Report a rate with its numerator, denominator and scope; avoid precision beyond the data. Known zero eligible units means **Not applicable — no eligible units**, not 0% success. An unobserved denominator means **Unknown**, even if some successes are known. Keep observed failure, abandonment, assistance, retry, blocked execution, missing outcome, withdrawal and not-run categories distinct. Apply the row's denominator literally; report missingness and its possible bias rather than silently deleting it. A retry does not replace an unfavorable first attempt or create a new person. For PB-03, inability to retrieve a sampled source is a failed traceability audit, not a convenient exclusion.

Time-to-completion is optional supporting evidence, measured only under a fixed task/timeout rule. A median among completers must accompany completion, failure and timeout counts; a faster survivor group is not proof that the task became easier. Qualitative explanations can identify a defect without proving its prevalence. A small purposive sample and a met threshold establish neither a population estimate nor a causal educational benefit. Publish measured associations and limitations at the actual scope, and reserve claims of validated improvement for a design and analysis that support them.

## 4. Minimal data, privacy and reproducible disclosure

For an engineering/source audit, retain public project references and the minimum execution/decision evidence needed to reproduce the stated result. A source account or commit author is not a deduplicated participant identifier. Do not enrich accounts with personal profiles or infer employment, protected characteristics, payer status or identity. PB-06 can establish a useful accepted contribution without collecting demographics or claiming a count of people.

For any later human task, ask only for context necessary to interpret that task. Functional access needs and the actual interaction method may matter; diagnoses, disability certificates, government identity documents and unrelated background do not belong in this instrument. The chosen platform and assistive technology may themselves produce personal data; verify the actual handling before use. W3C recommends combining user involvement with standards evaluation and cautions against generalizing from a few participants. The plan therefore preserves both the precise tested scope and unresolved barriers. [O4]

If Research #63 receives a measure, its current D-10/D-11/D-12 rules govern: written notes only, no public participant rows or quotations, aggregate-only disclosure with at least three distinct participants per cell **and** review of related totals and cross-release combinations. A count may be withheld without being zero. The floor is not an anonymity guarantee or a substitute for consent/rights review. Other canonical human studies must establish their applicable rules before collection; this document does not silently apply one pilot's authority to another population.

Do not place participant notes, contact/identity linkage, consent, individual hashes or private custody URLs in GitHub, artifacts, CI logs or this public template. The actual D-09 system must support D-10 deadlines across exports, caches, history, trash and backups. Its selected maxima include thirty days for pre-interview records, the earlier of ninety days after first interview or one hundred and twenty days after first opt-in for interview records, and earlier withdrawal/decline limits where applicable. **The canonical table and its clock/eligibility rules control; this summary is not a new retention schedule.** No storage system or deletion capability is verified here.

Remove withdrawn/expired contributions from continuing analysis and recompute affected counts without treating them as negative answers. A cleared public aggregate keeps its historical cutoff; it grants no new reuse of expired private rows. Publish only the permitted method, code/rubric, public or synthetic input, cleared aggregate and nonidentifying review receipt. Do not retain recoverable participant linkage in a supposedly anonymous audit record. If rights or disclosure are unresolved, report the claim's evidential limitation rather than upload its private support.

## 5. Practical use, effort and financial separation

An immediate **engineering-only** use is to select one already public, pinned evidence card, audit its claims with PB-03, record the method with the template, and give any real finding to its canonical owner. A synthetic walkthrough accompanies the template to check denominator handling before any collection. This plan itself performs no live product audit or participant study and runs no model, paid service, APK or physical-device experiment.

For a future task study, freeze the selected task and rubric, establish the actual protocol and resources, record baseline observations, implement the scoped correction in its owning repository, and collect only the comparable follow-up the protocol allows. Use PB-01/PB-02/PB-05 for human tasks, PB-03/PB-04 for evidence and reproduction, and PB-06/PB-07 for independently useful contributions and corrections. The matrix is an input to these owners, not a new implementation queue that replaces them.

**Effort method:** total person-hours = actual setup and instrument review + the sum of actual eligible session/audit/replay durations + coding/reconciliation + independent findings review + disclosure/deletion work. Record role, count, duration, source and uncertainty for each term; avoid double-counting a combined session. A hypothetical twenty sessions × forty-five minutes would require fifteen facilitator-hours of session time only. That arithmetic is not a recruited sample, availability commitment, total effort estimate or change to #63. Preparation, review, accessibility accommodations and administration remain additional **Unknown** amounts until scoped. Report proposed effort separately from logged effort.

**Value method:** report the actual number and scope of supported outcomes and the failure/uncertainty evidence. Monetary value is **Unknown** unless an independently defensible counterfactual and relevant observed inputs exist. Hours spent are not automatically wages, volunteer valuation or cash saved. Cash received, cash spent, activated usable credits, actual credit use and avoided cost are separate quantities. Avoided cost requires an evidenced alternative purchase that would otherwise occur, comparable scope and actual usage; a free tool, advertised grant ceiling or unused credit is not cash or realized savings.

The [needs ledger](needs-assessment.md), [budget issue #20](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/20) and [demand model #21](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/21) retain resource quantities and financial assumptions. An outcome claim should cite the accepted resource input and exact measurement window, without implying that support caused the outcome. Funding attribution requires its own evidence.
