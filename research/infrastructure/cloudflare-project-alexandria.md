## Cost-controlled public-artifact proposal

This is a **proposed procedure and synthetic sizing example**, not observed demand, a purchase plan or an external commitment.

1. **Prepare inputs offline.** Create an immutable manifest of at most ten intentionally public, rights-cleared artifacts, hashes, sizes, source revisions, audience and independent origin copies. Exclude credentials, private profiles, unredacted native logs and unresolved source-use material. Initially bound the candidate to 1GB aggregate storage and 30 days. These are reviewable design limits, not measured need.
2. **Select the smallest justified route.** Compare existing release/website delivery with static Pages and, only for an evidenced storage need, Standard R2. Do not enable Functions, an upgraded plan, Infrequent Access, Workers bindings or a domain transfer by default. Any extension gets its own unit model and receiving decision.
3. **Prepare the actual approval packet.** Establish applicant/account operator, project evidence, exact request, payment authority, service class, data terms, award currency/allocation/expiry, excluded charges and cancellation responsibility. Read the real current form and offer. Follow the accepted commitment procedure; do not precheck provider declarations.
4. **Bound owned operations and reconcile billing.** For the synthetic R2 month, propose at most 10,000 ClassA and 100,000 ClassB operations. A future implementation must count uploads, lists, reads and retries, stop its own requests at its approved bound, and reconcile real usage. These controls do not bound arbitrary public traffic, other account workloads or delayed billing; no provider hard spending cap is claimed.
5. **Prepare suspension and exit before release.** Retain independently retrievable origin bytes and routing configuration. The approved packet must name a real operator and review/stop date, address changed usage/scope, and require actual cancellation/export readback. Credit expiry or a disputed bill must not strand the sole evidence copy. This research assigns no person or arbitrary date.

[C10](#sources) prices Standard R2 by GB-month and separate write/list ClassA/read ClassB units. Monthly free allowances are 10GB-month, 1million ClassA and 10million ClassB, with chargeable units rounded upward. With **otherwise unused allowances in the same actual account**, the hypothetical 1GB/10,000A/100,000B month fits those allowances: its illustrative Standard R2 service charge is USD0 without sponsorship. This is a conditional calculation, not an invoice, total bill or free-account activation claim. Other services, taxes, existing allowance consumption and excess traffic are outside it. Infrequent Access has different minimum/retrieval conditions and is excluded.

No positive credit request or avoided-cost value follows from that example. Value an actual award only after measuring eligible incremental units and identifying the lowest-cost feasible alternative. Keep requested credits, funded capacity, cash spending and realized avoided cost separate; deduct evidenced migration, monitoring, export and maintenance effort. Unused allocations have no demonstrated realized value. Actual workload, effort, award, counterfactual cost and net value remain **Unknown**. All weighted scoring factors and the total stay null; P2 expresses a relevant but incompletely qualified option, not a manufactured likelihood or money ranking.

## Rights, obligations and independence

The [self-serve agreement C3](#sources) requires authority to bind the entity, incorporates service terms and authorizes billing for paid use. Paid subscriptions renew unless canceled before the next billing date. Content ownership remains with the customer alongside the service license and necessary third-party permissions; falsely implying sponsorship is prohibited. Its §2.2.2 permits benchmarking with reproducibility disclosures and reciprocal provider benchmarking rights. These are material published conditions, not program-specific permission for a study. An actual enterprise/negotiated agreement may govern a different route and must be read.

Project controls are separate decisions: preserve independent evaluation selection/results and sponsorship disclosure; accept no undisclosed editorial influence. Acknowledgment text, publicity consent, reporting, renewal evidence and badges remain **Unknown** until the actual offer. Silence on the landing page does not prove absence of obligations.

[C4](#sources) distinguishes website/contact, account and end-user information and describes purpose-dependent retention; it does not establish our settings or zero retention. [C9](#sources) permits limits/suspension and makes the customer responsible for its content, licenses and users. Default deployment names can change. Possible access for up to 30 days after termination is **not guaranteed retention or an export window**. Independent copies and rollback planning are engineering inferences, not observed recovery results.

Before a real request, resolve custody, content/publication scope, tax/payment/paid-fallback responsibility, sponsor restrictions, current form declarations, exact coverage and cancellation/export handling. Internal questions can be prepared from this list; none were sent. This assessment supplies no organizational appointment, agreement, account access or rights grant.
