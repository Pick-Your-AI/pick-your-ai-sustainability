## 4. Proposed learning and accessibility methods

### E1 — education research question, not an executed study

**Proposed question:** for an explicitly defined adult audience, does a fixed evidence-reading lesson change performance on an independently specified task of distinguishing supported claims, assumptions and unavailable evidence? This is a research concept to examine with a qualified investigator, not a ready protocol or an asserted effect.

The intervention could use a frozen, plainly labeled synthetic comparison and a rights-cleared real-source excerpt or summary. A task would ask the reader to identify one unsupported conclusion, select a warranted uncertainty statement and trace one supported field. The question is about understanding, not willingness to buy, model speed, universally correct procurement or a clinical outcome. Which lesson version, audience, comparison condition, allocation, masking, sampling, duration, follow-up, validated instrument and primary analysis are defensible remains **Unknown**.

The actual investigator would first justify the study question, relevant educational literature, method and sample design. A preregistered randomized comparison might be appropriate in some settings, but this screen selects no design or sample size and claims no power calculation. Relevant consent, accessibility, site/institutional and ethics decisions must precede the corresponding protected activity. The absence of an approval requirement at one proposal stage does not authorize research execution. [S6 for Spencer's published integrity framework]

### E2 — public-engagement exhibit, not a measured effect

**Proposed artifact:** a self-contained, accessible explanation that lets a reader inspect the source, date and limits of one comparison claim; a synthetic example makes missing evidence visibly different from failure or a measured value. Include a text alternative, keyboard path and clear labels for the synthetic inputs. Select the exact implementation and source-use permissions through the existing product and Research owners, not through this funding note.

The New Media concept's outputs could be the exhibit, a rights-cleared explanatory text, a public correction process and a bounded accessibility review. Its outcomes would remain hypotheses until observed. Distribution impressions, source downloads, automated accessibility checks, repository stars and a release are not interchangeable with comprehension or successful use by disabled participants. If a project combines non-research engagement with substantive research, Sloan's guidelines direct it to the **research** proposal route; the non-research packet is not an exemption from the appropriate method or review. [S10]
