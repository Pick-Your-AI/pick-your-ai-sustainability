# Program roadmap

Status: planning sequence. The 30/60/90-day horizons begin only from an explicitly accepted program kickoff; they create no issue deadlines, assignees or application commitments.

| Horizon | Intended work | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 30 days | Establish entity/authority baseline, canonical records, current needs, existing free capabilities, initial qualified support and legal decision questions | Reviewed baseline, source-linked register, auditable priorities, duplicate reconciliation and a useful research queue |
| Days 31–60 | Resolve feasible structural choices, prepare application evidence and resource-specific proposals; conduct only authorized outreach/application work | Concrete authority records, verified eligibility/terms, usable budget and independent review |
| Days 61–90 | Activate any properly approved benefits, meet actual obligations, review risks and renew the pipeline | Receipt/activation evidence, accepted reporting, renewal controls, remaining-needs and independence review |

The nine epics may progress in parallel where their exact inputs permit it. Drafting templates need not wait for incorporation. An application that requires a legal applicant, sponsor or recognition must wait for the applicable evidence and authorization.

Prioritize foundational work and demonstrably useful existing no-cost support. P0 is reserved for a verified urgent deadline or serious operational blocker. No program opening, successful application or resource availability is promised by this roadmap.

The monthly review in [research-freshness.md](research-freshness.md) updates the sequence from evidence. A changed opportunity or expired source may change priority; it does not rewrite the historical record.
