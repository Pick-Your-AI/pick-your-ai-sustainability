# Program charter

Status: initial draft for independent review. Prepared 2026-10-19.

## Purpose

Identify and qualify support that helps Pick Your AI remain accessible, evidence-based, open and independent. Research includes cash, inference and GPU access, cloud infrastructure, physical hardware, software, security reviews, professional services and institutional assistance.

The candidate public benefits are education about AI architecture and costs, transparent comparisons, reproducible research, interoperability, accessibility and community participation. The [organization profile](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md) and [research scope](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/main/README.md), inspected 2026-10-19, support the direction; they do not establish charitable qualification or achieved impact.

## Authority and scope

Research, draft documentation, issue management and source verification are within the program's execution assignment. Applications, messages to prospective sponsors, financial accounts, legal filings, agreements, spending and commitments require the applicable concrete authorization. An approval identifies its issuer, scope, artifacts and limits; a role title alone is insufficient.

This repository owns support research and operating records. The existing technical repositories retain product, contract, evaluation, release and security decisions. Organization-wide governance remains canonical in [.github](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/.github).

## Qualification paths

| Path | Required assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Immediate | Does this specific program admit the project's actual current status? |
| Fiscal sponsor | Is the selected sponsor arrangement accepted by the program, with clear applicant, control and obligations? |
| Independent nonprofit | Does actual recognition and every additional jurisdiction/program requirement establish eligibility? |

Eligibility is a gate before an opportunity is actionable. Research about a blocked opportunity can still be useful. No sponsor may purchase favorable rankings, research results, compatibility conclusions or exclusion of competitors.

## Success and governance

Success is evidence-backed support that can be used responsibly for demonstrated needs. Report research accepted, applications authorized/submitted, support awarded/received/activated, realized avoided costs and obligations independently. Retain unsuccessful findings so the community does not repeat them.

Program coordinator, financial authority, legal reviewer, independent research reviewer and operating owner are required roles, currently unassigned in this draft. Actual identities and mandates must be recorded through E01.01 and E09.02. The charter becomes adopted only through its recorded review and appropriate organizational decision.
