## 1. Outcome, recommendation and limits

This is a **proposed support-model menu for independent engineering review**. It separates unrestricted support, restricted support, acknowledgment, commercial return benefits and noncash support. It defines what each model could fund, which benefits are permissible candidates, which conditions require refusal or revision, and what evidence is needed before a real arrangement. No tier amount is proposed. Actual delivery cost, available capacity, cash need, applicant eligibility and agreement authority remain Unknown where the underlying records do not establish them.

The recommended disposition is **accept this bounded menu as a design input for #37 after independent source acceptance; keep all offers and activations unresolved**. The [accepted draft neutrality policy](../policies/vendor-neutrality-and-sponsorship-ethics-draft.md) supplies the constraints. Its actual [source acceptance](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/pull/106#pullrequestreview-5572715412) and [signed-main completion receipt](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/18#issuecomment-6269578885) satisfy #35's research prerequisite at that draft scope. They do not adopt organizational policy, appoint a financial decision-maker or authorize a sponsor agreement.

This work makes no sponsorship commitment, solicitation, application, account enrollment, published tier, invoice, tax acknowledgment, payment, benefit activation or outreach. It changes no provider program record or another repository's implementation. These are statements about this authored work, not an assertion that every other participant has taken no action.

The issue is P1 foundational work: it prevents a later kit or channel setup from conflating donations with sales or purchasing research influence. A weighted opportunity score, award probability and application deadline are **Not applicable** to this internal design. No separate unseen founding brief is asserted as an input. The current published issue, charter, accepted draft policy and named repository records below are the governing source basis.

The [workflow and authorization procedure](../workflow-and-authorization.md) distinguishes research completion from actual execution. Reuse an existing valid authorization when its real issuer, action, party, version, limits and continuing conditions cover the next step. Ordinary authorized source work does not require another permission request. A role name or this review cannot supply missing authority for a different external action.

## 3. Classification before a menu choice

Classify the **actual substance and every promised return**, not the payer's label. A single packet can contain multiple classes. Record each amount or native unit, restriction, return benefit, party, term and payment state separately; link the combined agreement so splitting a line does not conceal its conditions.

| Class | Meaning for this menu | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unrestricted support | A contribution proposed for the independently governed public-benefit work, with no purchased service or result. | Individual versus corporate identity does not itself decide tax treatment. Do not promise deductibility or call an unverified recipient a recognized charity. |
| Restricted gift or grant | Funds limited to a genuinely independently selected public-benefit purpose, period or eligible cost set. | A donor restriction is not permission to select findings, suppress failures or exclude competitors. A requested service or material return may require a different or mixed classification. |
| Acknowledgment-based support | A proposed arrangement with a bounded, factual acknowledgment. | Name/logo permission, expected return benefits and actual terms need separate review. Neither “sponsor” nor a value-neutral draft automatically establishes an IRS classification. |
| Commercial return benefit / earned income | Payment buys a specified, permitted deliverable or service. | Record commercial cost and obligations distinctly from any gift portion. Delivery cost is not automatically the fair market value used for any applicable receipt treatment. |
| Noncash gift, service, credit or loan | Particular eligible goods, services or use rights, sometimes with return or expiry obligations. | Property donated is different from property lent; credits are different from cash; realized avoided cost is different from face value or a provider's list price. |

The proposed policy can exclude a benefit even when a platform permits it or a legal classification might be available. IRS S1–S3 and FTC S4 below supply limited official context; this menu makes no organization-specific legal, tax or donor determination.

### Benefits excluded from every model

The following are proposed refusal or revision conditions, not benefits that can be cured merely by disclosure:

- Purchased rankings, compatibility or readiness decisions, favorable conclusions, selected evidence, changed thresholds, competitor exclusions or suppression of failures.
- Sponsor approval of methodology, reviewer selection, publication outcome or public correction; an unlimited or outcome-contingent review embargo.
- Preferential research admission, review queues, access to nonpublic findings, or influence over unrelated procurement. Funding a finite public task does not buy a vendor a better evidentiary standard.
- Endorsements, naming that suggests independent certification, undisclosed material connections, or control over an equivalent competitor acknowledgment.
- Donor, user or participant lists, lead generation, private telemetry, access credentials, confidential evidence, or material for which the actual recipient lacks the needed use/publication rights.
- Unbounded support, automatic spend/overage exposure, hidden renewal obligations, or conditions that prevent reasonable independent exit and truthful record retention.

Affiliate arrangements, advertising inventory and exclusive-provider deals are **not selected models in this menu**. Any future proposal needs its own explicit substantive review; S3/S4 context and an existing platform feature do not introduce it here.

## 7. Amounts, effort, limits and value

### 7.1 No proposed tier price

All six current amount fields are **Unknown / no amount proposed**. This is deliberate: the received budget contains planning scenarios, not verified marginal delivery costs, operating balances or sponsor willingness. The historical USD 112.70 monthly engineering scenario and its USD 200 alternative ceiling must not be added together, adopted as an organizational budget or converted into tier prices. Accepted need identification supplies a place to investigate cost, not a quote. [Budget source](../budget-assumptions.md).

For M01/M02, a voluntary unrestricted contribution is not a priced purchase. If a future channel requires a preset contribution level, record the actual channel rule and why the suggested amount is appropriate; do not pretend it was derived from willingness to pay or promises of research access. For M03–M06, no amount or valuation becomes publishable until the particular scope and route have the applicable evidence below.

### 7.2 Reproducible cost and capacity method

For one explicitly bounded scope, currency and period, document:

1. Exact deliverable, eligible workload, output/license limits, actual dependency owners and independently chosen public-benefit purpose.
2. Nonduplicated incremental purchases, with actual quotes or defensible current prices and eligibility. Exclude already-free, already-funded and mutually alternative resources. Existing sunk costs and marginal costs are labeled separately.
3. Work units by task: intake/clauses, technical delivery, independent review, accounting, acknowledgment maintenance, reporting and exit. Estimate hours from stated analogous observations or a justified work breakdown; preserve the estimate's uncertainty and actual record separately.
4. Actual available capacity after existing commitments. An estimate of needed hours is not a volunteer commitment or evidence of availability. Any cash labor rate needs its own defensible basis; volunteer hours are not silently monetized or booked as cash.
5. Actual fixed and variable channel/host/payment charges, their calculation bases, applicable taxes, currency conversion and uncovered recurring costs. Unknown fees do not become zero; percentages are not simply added when their bases differ.
6. A separately justified risk allowance, if one is actually approved, and explicit limits/expiry. No arbitrary margin or contingency is introduced by this menu.

Let `C` be the evidenced nonduplicated incremental cash cost, `H_i` the justified hours for task `i`, and `r_i` a supported cash rate when applicable. The proposed cash delivery cost is `C + sum(H_i × r_i)` only for actual payable labor; retain noncash time separately. Capacity acceptance independently requires that this scope fits the real available hours and obligations. Missing critical inputs leave the result Unknown.

For a future commercial arrangement, a break-even expression is useful **only if the actual terms define its variables**. If `A` is that accepted cash delivery cost, `R` a separately approved cash allowance, `F` a fixed charge in the same currency/period and `v` a single verified fraction charged on the gross amount `P`, then `net available = P × (1 − v) − F`; covering the stated costs requires `P ≥ (A + R + F) / (1 − v)` for `0 ≤ v < 1`. This is algebra, not a proposed price, fee rate, tax rule or permission to sell. Multiple fee bases, caps, taxes, foreign currencies, refunds or other obligations require their actual formulas instead. Do not round an amount or publish a price until the currency/minor-unit and actual terms are known.

A future proposed amount must disclose its method, source dates, uncertainty, benefit classification and capacity evidence, and remain unapproved until the actual competent decision. It must not be represented as the fair market return-benefit value or a donor's deductible amount merely because it covers delivery cost.

### 7.3 Keep cash, noncash and benefit outcomes separate

| Quantity | Evidence and method | Invalid substitution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offered / committed / awarded / received cash | Separate actual event, currency, restrictions, date and cleared evidence; gross receipt, fees and usable balance remain distinct. | A pledge or expected award is not available cash; Unknown is not zero. |
| Eligible noncash capacity | Exact native units, account/offering/region/feature/data eligibility, expiry, usable workload and actual consumption. | Headline credit, list price or unused/free allowance is not realized savings. |
| Realized avoided cost | A documented necessary alternative purchase that would otherwise have occurred, matched to actual eligible consumed units, less actual incremental operating/exit costs on a comparable basis. | Do not count both cash funding and the same avoided purchase, or add alternative platforms' face values. Negative net value stays visible. |
| Noncash work or donated/loaned property | Actual hours or property/use units and ownership/return status, with a separately qualified valuation only when needed. | A service donation, equipment loan and property gift are not interchangeable cash contributions. |
| Public-benefit outcome | Use the accepted measurement plan's declared population, numerator/denominator, period, missingness, privacy and attribution. | Commits, test counts, funds received or reports written are not proof of participant benefit, deployment success or causal impact. |

### 7.4 Exposure and concentration limits

Each actionable future arrangement needs a finite **amount/native-unit, time, benefit and workload limit** based on actual authority and evidence. “One standard acknowledgment location for the reviewed term” is a bounded design candidate; its actual location, rights, capacity and end date still need to be supplied. Do not assume “unlimited support,” a production SLA, perpetual logo display or an arbitrary number of sponsor slots.

Use the accepted risk method for sponsor/host/provider concentration on the declared actual population and period. Keep restricted funds, commercial receipts and noncash dependencies distinguishable; no invented threshold or merged denomination makes them equivalent. An independent decision may revise or refuse an arrangement whose control or exit exposure is unacceptable even if its estimated net cost is favorable.
