## 6. Value equations and Unknown propagation

For one admitted scope, period and currency, define:

- `C`: complete reference-priced workload cost at the supplied rates.
- `F`: applicable support face value.
- `E`: evidenced eligible-spend cap for this workload and product.
- `P`: evidenced spend that can fit before expiry, after capacity and schedule constraints.

The accepted method's **potential useful coverage** is `U = min(C, F, E, P)` when eligibility, currency and all material inputs are established. A missing cap is Unknown, not an unlimited cap. A known ineligible scope or explicit zero pre-expiry cap can establish zero potential coverage under the accepted calculator's rules; this does not make the excluded route a candidate. Cross-currency coverage remains Unknown without a separately qualified method.

`C - U` is **uncovered list exposure**, not an invoice or an authorized overage amount. The independent `cash_quote` must establish actual payable cash, its billing scope, discounts, fees, taxes, excluded meters and complete-period treatment. Cash received, a service credit and an avoided cost are different kinds of evidence; none should be added to another as if all were cash revenue.

For a completed, sourced realization with actual activation and eligible matching-currency expenditure, let `B` be the necessary counterfactual cash purchase, `A` the actual cash expenditure covered by support within its evidenced caps, and `I` the incremental cash caused by accepting and operating that support. Then:

```text
gross avoided cash = min(B, A)
net avoided cash   = min(B, A) - I
```

The net may be negative. Missing completion, activation, necessary counterfactual purchase, covered cash or incremental cost leaves actual avoided cash Unknown. Existing free capability and an unnecessary purchase cannot be presented as money saved. Labor hours, cash cost, risk and scientific utility must stay separate unless their conversion is explicitly justified; this document assigns no hourly wage or probability of receiving support.

### Reproducible hypothetical sensitivity control

**Every amount below is invented, in one fictional currency unit (CU), for one fictional workload horizon.** Offers A and B are not real providers or programs. Their gates are assumed satisfied solely for the arithmetic control. Neither their amounts nor their output is a project estimate, a calculator input update, an award or realized avoided cash.

A has face value 1,000 CU, eligible-spend cap 80 CU, pre-expiry cap 20 CU and hypothetical incremental implementation cash 5 CU. B has face value 100 CU, eligible-spend cap 80 CU, normal pre-expiry cap 70 CU and incremental implementation cash 15 CU.

Keep potential reference-valued coverage `U` and hypothetical incremental implementation cash `H` as **separate dimensions**: greater coverage and lower incremental cash are preferable only with the common task, quality, rights and all other relevant conditions held equal. There is no assumed cash equivalence between a unit of reference-priced support and a unit of payable or avoided cash. Do not subtract H from U, or infer a total economic ordering from these two columns. No actual payable quote, counterfactual purchase or support realization is supplied.

| Hypothetical case | Reference C | B's pre-expiry cap | Coverage U(A) / U(B) | Incremental cash H(A) / H(B) | Consequence on these two dimensions only |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Low demand | 20 CU | 70 CU | 20 / 20 | 5 / 15 | A weakly dominates: equal coverage, lower hypothetical cash. |
| Base demand | 80 CU | 70 CU | 20 / 70 | 5 / 15 | B offers higher coverage with higher hypothetical cash; no total ordering. |
| B expires sooner | 80 CU | 10 CU | 20 / 10 | 5 / 15 | A dominates: higher coverage and lower hypothetical cash. |
| B's schedule unproven | 80 CU | Unknown | 20 / Unknown | 5 / 15 | Coverage comparison remains unresolved; cash alone does not settle the choice. |

Copy the following into a local Python 3 standard-library session. It performs only exact arithmetic and makes no file, network or provider call:

```python
from fractions import Fraction as Q

def coverage(reference, face, eligible, before_expiry):
    values = (reference, face, eligible, before_expiry)
    if any(value is None for value in values):
        return None
    return min(Q(str(value)) for value in values)

def display(value):
    return "Unknown" if value is None else str(value)

cases = [
    ("low", 20, 70),
    ("base", 80, 70),
    ("earlier-expiry", 80, 10),
    ("unknown-schedule", 80, None),
]
for name, reference, b_expiry in cases:
    a = coverage(reference, 1000, 80, 20)
    b = coverage(reference, 100, 80, b_expiry)
    print(name, *(display(v) for v in (a, b, Q(5), Q(15))))
```

Expected output, in columns case, U(A), U(B), H(A), H(B):

```text
low 20 20 5 15
base 20 70 5 15
earlier-expiry 20 10 5 15
unknown-schedule 20 Unknown 5 15
```

A false permission or eligibility gate would remove the corresponding offer before this calculation; it is not another numerical sensitivity. Actual project avoided cash remains Unknown in every row.
