**Draft for review — no legal structure adopted, filings made, directors appointed or agreement accepted.** Prepared 2026-10-19. Owning research: [E01-03 #13](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/13); baseline: [E01-01 #11](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/11); purpose assessment: [E01-02 #12](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/12).

## Decision to prepare

Choose a structure only after establishing who presently owns and controls the project, what activity actually exists, what support must be administered and who is authorized to decide. Pick Your AI’s intention to pursue nonprofit status is established by the program brief. Its current incorporation, EIN, exemption, jurisdiction, board, financial accounts and fiscal-host agreements remain Unknown until evidence is accepted.

The IRS distinguishes state nonprofit status from federal tax exemption. Its preparation sequence calls for organizational documents, state registration analysis and an EIN; those steps do not themselves prove recognized exemption. See [IRS preparation guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/before-applying-for-tax-exempt-status) and [recognition application routes](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/applying-for-tax-exempt-status), checked 2026-10-19.

## Paths to compare

| Path | What it could solve | Evidence needed before a recommendation | Material limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continue current community operation while researching | Preserve existing contribution work while inventorying authority and needs | Current owners, account/contract facts, intended near-term work | No unsupported charitable, tax or organizational claims |
| Become a member project of a charitable sponsor such as SFC | Hosted administration, assets and contracts for an accepted mission | Program fit, maturity, governance, licensing and a reviewed agreement | Membership changes the organizational relationship and control of hosted assets |
| Associate with a charitable fiscal sponsor such as SPI | Administration of funds and contracts through the sponsor | Project significance, liaison and service needs, approval/transfer terms | Associated project retains its legal identity; sponsor status is not automatically the project’s status |
| Use a noncharitable nonprofit fiscal host such as OSC | Hosted fundraising and payments without creating a separate entity first | Current acceptance, fee, payment and governance terms | 501(c)(6) hosting does not establish charitable sponsorship or the project’s own 501(c)(3) status |
| Form and maintain an independent recognized charity | Potential independent governance and access to programs requiring own recognition | Suitable jurisdiction, purpose and operating tests, governing documents, decision authority, full recurring administration model | Incorporation, EIN, federal recognition and individual provider validation are separate gates |

The host relationships above are supported by [SFC member services](https://sfconservancy.org/projects/services/), [SPI’s relationship definition](https://www.spi-inc.org/projects/relationship/) and [OSC’s description](https://oscollective.org/about/), checked 2026-10-19. The [host comparison](fiscal-sponsorship-comparison.md) and [opportunity register](../../data/opportunities.json) retain specific source terms and Unknowns.

## Recommended research sequence

1. Complete the public-safe entity/asset/authority ledger and the purpose-to-actual-activity assessment. Keep private documents behind references, not in public issues.
2. Establish the resources the next defensible milestones require. Count actual adopted work and scoped needs; distinguish synthetic website examples, unsigned mobile drafts, research contracts and a planned leaderboard service from released user-facing capability.
3. Compare the three named hosts in parallel with independent formation. Determine which target support programs accept each route and which require their own charitable status or an academic applicant.
4. Model administration, control of funds, fees, accounting, insurance where relevant, intellectual-property custody and exit. Use Unknown for costs or obligations not established by current terms.
5. Present a decision packet to the actual authorized people. Include the proposed path, evidence, alternatives, conflicts, unresolved legal questions, implementation sequence and review/approval records. No person is assigned by this draft.

This sequence is a reversible research recommendation. It is not a recommendation to apply, incorporate or accept a host before the evidence and decision gates are satisfied.

## Questions the final decision must answer

- What specific public educational, scientific or charitable activities are supported by existing artifacts? What is still proposed?
- Which assets, domains, contracts, data and funds would transfer, and which would stay with existing owners?
- Who can approve spending, sign contracts, make tax representations and grant software/account access under each route?
- Which host obligations continue on exit, and what happens if leadership changes or the project ceases?
- What recurring work must the project perform, who has agreed to perform it and what evidence supports the cost?
- Which sources of support would be usable after fees, restrictions, delivery work and future operating costs?

## Acceptance and completion

The owning issue can close only after the comparison is evidence-backed, uncertainty is explicit and an independent reviewer accepts the research. Selecting a legal structure is a later authorized organizational decision. Filing, recognition, hosting acceptance, program eligibility and funding acquisition require their own evidence and are never inferred from this document’s completion.
