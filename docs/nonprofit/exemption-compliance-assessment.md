For Form 990-N, “normally” uses age-sensitive rules: up to one year old, receipts including pledges of no more than USD 75,000 in the first tax year; over one but under three years old, an average of no more than USD 60,000 over the first two tax years; and at least three years old, no more than USD 50,000 averaged over the relevant three tax years, including the current one.
