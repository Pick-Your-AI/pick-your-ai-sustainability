## Concrete use case and value/effort method

**Proposed public-benefit deliverable:** a versioned educational guide that helps a reader distinguish measured AI results, documented specifications, estimates and unresolved claims, with accessible examples and reproducible source notes. It would support the existing research/website/leaderboard evidence work and mobile presentation needs without creating a new competing implementation lane. A hypothetical grant could support rights-cleared documentation, accessibility work or independently scoped evaluation costs if the actual charity, grant and needs assessment permitted them.

The proposal is not evidence of demand, educational impact, accessibility conformance, staff availability or a charitable grant decision. Do not invent users, testimonials, surveys or measured outcomes. Any later delivery should identify its owning issue, exact source, acceptance checks, recipient's permitted charitable purpose, cost items and operating capacity before requesting funding. No service subscription, paid API, hardware purchase or person is appointed here.

Estimate usable cash only when there is actual evidence: **settled funds available to the actual recipient or project under the host arrangement, less applicable documented charges, restricted or refundable amounts and unavoidable supported delivery/administration costs**. Record currency and relevant FX separately. Avoid double subtraction when a statement already reports net payment, and avoid treating the DAF sponsor's donor-account expenses as recipient fees. An advertised donor minimum or market-wide giving total is not a project forecast. Matching support counts only after its own actual decision and receipt evidence, without duplicating a pooled disbursement.

Avoided cost needs a different counterfactual: a specific necessary purchase the project would otherwise make, covered by an actual in-kind benefit. None of these researched access routes establishes that counterfactual. Potential distribution reach or convenience is not cash. Leave estimated usable value and the eight weighted factors null until the eligibility and evidence gates are met; use the supplied 20/20/15/10/10/10/10/5 method only after that.

Effort categories are recipient/host qualification, authoritative profile and rights preparation, terms/privacy/payment review, actual need and delivery scoping, reconciliation, donor-consent handling, reporting and exit. Hours, owner availability and costs require actual estimates. **P2** reflects relevant charitable-support routes with unresolved recipient and demand facts, not a success probability or application-ready recommendation.
