## 2. Program findings and priority rationale

### Georgia Tech CS Junior Design — GATECH-CS-JUNIOR-DESIGN

The official client page describes a two-semester external-client software course, normally teams of five. Participation has no client program charge, while client-supplied project resources remain the client's expense. A client provides a short proposal and contact, participates in at least three or four meetings per semester, and receives progress and end-of-term presentations. Selection is not guaranteed; neither warranty nor continued maintenance is promised. Students retain their new software IP and choose its open-source license; existing client material stays with its owner. A possible small SLS community-partner stipend is conditional, unquantified and not counted as project income. The directly linked charter was unread and the submission page exposed only an embedded shell. [G1], [G4], [G5]

The separate student description supports requirements, design, implementation, technical communication and handoff work across two semesters. [G2] This is the strongest initial fit for a modest offline interface, provided a real maintainer can supervise and the selected license fits the receiving repository. The program's recurring selection-week descriptions do not establish a current cohort cutoff. The home page's empty post list does not prove the program closed. [G3]

**Disposition:** P2 prepare; defer submission until actual client authority, mentoring, course availability, expense ceiling and full charter/license terms are received. This is not a free production-engineering or free-hosting entitlement.

### UW iSchool MSIM Capstone — UW-ISCHOOL-MSIM-CAPSTONE

It accepts ideas year-round and labels its general calendar representative; no binding current cohort cutoff, nonprofit fee, waiver or refund rule was established. The FAQ requires an organization rather than an outside personal project. Its optional student-compensation discussion does not negate the dated program fee. [U2] The selected **MSIM** page describes autumn charter/agreement preparation, winter design and spring implementation, teams of at most four, and a restriction on simultaneous same-sponsor internships. **Disposition:** P2 conditional information-management design route. Its documented research/design fit is useful for evidence traceability and explanation. An actual organization, accountable mentor, resource permissions, verified fee treatment and agreed publication/license terms are required. The inaccessible intake form and unavailable agreement remain decision gates. Do not borrow Informatics matching, MLIS handbook contents or historical human-subjects rules for this MSIM proposal.

The SCS program comparison identifies the Scalable Systems/Embedded Systems practicum as a fall course and advertises **$13,000 per project, or $10,000 for nonprofits**. [C3] Only the linked form's first organization-information page was readable; no later page or agreement was accepted. [C4]

**Disposition:** P3 defer a paid engagement until independently accepted need and capacity show why this course outperforms a smaller internal or other-course alternative.
