# Benefit and obligation report

Use the [operating procedure](../docs/operations-and-reporting.md) and the linked [benefit/obligation record](benefit-obligation-record.md). **Default: Draft / not dispatched.** This record does not establish an award, terms acceptance, received support, completed output or permission to report.

## Benefits and financial reconciliation

| Measure | Planned or awarded | Actually received, used or settled | Method/evidence and unresolved difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash by fund/custodian/currency | Unknown | Unknown | Gross, fees, net, restrictions and actual statement/control references |
| Opening + inflows − outflows = closing cash | Unknown | Unknown | Reconciliation and independent result; no balancing plug |
| Reservations / liabilities / payable / reimbursement | Unknown | Unknown | Separate commitments, actual settlements, duplicate and reversal checks |
| Credits / software entitlement | Unknown | Unknown | Units or seats awarded, available, consumed, expired and remaining |
| Hardware gift or loan / services | Unknown | Unknown | Title/custody/return or promised/delivered/accepted scope |
| Estimated avoided cost | Unknown | Not a realized result | Comparable useful scope, quantity, cost and explicit assumptions |
| Realized avoided cost | Not inferred from award | Unknown | Actual eligible use, supported alternative purchase, incremental charges, currency/date and review |

Do not add credit face value, loaned property price, donated-service estimates or avoided cost to received cash. Do not combine currencies or custodians without the accepted financial-control basis. Unknown actual receipt/use remains Unknown, not zero. Preserve actual charges, fees, refunds and remaining liabilities even when the net amount is lower.

## Planned versus delivered outcomes

One row per original deliverable and period; link the actual artifact/version and original evidence.

| Deliverable / agreed acceptance measure | Planned scope and period | Actual result and exact output | Independent review and remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unknown | Unknown | Unknown; explicitly Pass, Fail, Partial or Not run only when evidenced | Unassigned / Unknown |

Retain unsuccessful or unexecuted work and original evidence. Separate a provider's acceptance standard from a technical test result. An approved plan is not a delivered output; a passing subset is not completion of the full scope. Explain variance, remaining work and proposed versus actually accepted deviations, with exact amendment and authority references.
