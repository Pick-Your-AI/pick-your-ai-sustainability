## Three independent state records

Keep these separate:

1. **Research work:** issue status and exact independent acceptance. Done research may retain a Not Pursuing disposition.
2. **Opportunity lifecycle:** the existing register values Discovered, Verified, Qualified, Preparing, Submitted, Awarded, Declined, Activated and Reporting, supported by actual events.
3. **Operation and obligation records:** each benefit's usable state, each attempted action, each report or return, and item-specific closeout. Draft, Blocked, Pending, Confirmed or Closed labels in these records do not add lifecycle values to the canonical register.

Store the prior and resulting lifecycle value, event date, evidence date, exact event/version, authorized recorder and independent transition review in an append-only history. Do not manufacture intermediate events to make a linear funnel. An unsolicited documented offer may establish Awarded without an application; record why Submitted is not applicable. A bad gate, lapsed entitlement or later rejection does not erase the actual earlier award, use or report.

### Transition and authorization table

| Change or retained state | Evidence required | Authorization and blocked result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New identity → Discovered | Distinct existing-or-new program identity, aliases/round and a dated source lead; duplicate search. | Existing research scope permits recording it. No offer, applicant fit or operating value inferred. |
| Discovered → Verified | Independently accepted canonical program/intake/terms assessment; exact revision, reviewer, source dates, limits and Unknowns. | Source checking alone cannot perform this transition. Verified is program research, not actual applicant qualification. |
| Verified → Qualified | Specific recipient/route and eligible use, capacity, restrictions, defensible value/effort inputs and an attributable qualification decision. | Missing applicant or material evidence keeps the qualification gate open; another route's result cannot fill it. |
| Qualified → Preparing | Concrete preparation scope and evidence checklist under actual existing or new preparation authority. | A draft or approval to prepare is not permission to dispatch. **Approved to apply is an action decision, not a new lifecycle stage.** |
| Preparing → Submitted | Exact actual application/attachments, authorized submitter, time/channel and confirmed provider submission receipt. | An approval or attempted/uncertain dispatch is not confirmed submission. Reconcile uncertainty before retry. |
| Submitted or documented unsolicited offer → Awarded | Actual provider decision for the identified applicant/program/round, benefit and conditions; actual event evidence. | **Terms acceptance is separate.** An award with unaccepted terms stays Awarded with activation Blocked; do not claim receipt or spendable funds. |
| Submitted or other documented decision → Declined | Actual provider decision and its scope/reason, including a partial decision's separate accepted/rejected portions. | Preserve source and submission history. A project's Not Pursuing recommendation is not a provider decline. |
| Awarded → Activated | Accepted exact terms; applicable recipient/account, operating mandate and financial controls; **confirmed usable access, reconciled receipt or accepted delivery** for the identified benefit. | All applicable common and type-specific checks below must pass. Account creation, shipping notice, a balance screenshot without identity, or an offer alone is insufficient. |
| Activated → Reporting | Actual agreement-derived obligation and reporting period/trigger; actual reporting activity and its evidence. | A report draft, dispatch, provider acknowledgment and provider acceptance remain separate facts. An obligation due before activation is tracked at the true current lifecycle without inventing an activation event. |
| Any state with new facts, failed gate or changed conditions | Exact change, source, affected scope and review; preserve every original event. | Record the current blocker or Not Pursuing disposition; suspend only the uncovered action. Retain unrelated valid authorization and outcomes. |
| Renewal, return or closeout | Term-specific decision and item-level evidence in the operating record below. | These are not new lifecycle enum values. Closed applies only to verified obligations; historical Awarded/Activated/Reporting evidence remains. |

A single provider relationship may contain cash, licenses and a loan with different activation dates and open obligations. Use linked benefit records and a truthful aggregate description; one usable component must not stand for the whole offer.

## Authority, common activation checks and role coverage

The actual recipient, action approver, any signer/host, operator/custodian, reporting owner and independent reviewer must accept their applicable scope. Reuse the accepted #68/#24 role separation and conflict rules. A research reviewer is not appointed as a billing owner; a repository role is not a payment mandate. An unfilled required role blocks its action.

Before an authorized operator activates a real benefit:

1. Bind the existing opportunity ID, selected applicant route, actual recipient/contracting party, benefit ID, offer, agreement and amendments to exact versions. Distinguish reviewed terms from actually accepted terms and acceptance evidence.
2. Link the exact applicable approval, mandate, limits and continuing conditions. Resolve material changes; submission or negotiation authority must not be silently expanded into acceptance, spending or renewal.
3. Confirm the measurable permitted use with its existing technical owner and acceptance criteria. Record restrictions, data rights, publication/benchmark rights, branding, independence, expiry, recurring charges, maximum exposure and exit conditions.
4. Verify actual eligible fund/custody and #24 controls where money or liability is involved. An alert is not a hard cap; if exposure cannot be bounded within authority, leave activation blocked until an effective control or explicitly approved exposure exists.
5. Record the planned non-destructive verification, its scope and actual result: recipient/account or asset identity, available entitlement, permitted access/use and any charged amount. Do not execute a paid or binding verification without its covered authority.
6. Link actual operator/custodian acceptance, continuing obligation owner and independent review of the complete activation evidence. Record failed, partial and uncertain results as such. Reconcile provider/custodian state before a repeat when the first attempt's effect is uncertain.

## Benefit ledger and reconciliation

Use the [benefit record](../templates/benefit-obligation-record.md) linked to the actual #24 control record. Separate award/commitment, receipt/availability, use and value fields. Empty or unknown evidence is **Unknown**, not zero. Zero is permitted only as an explicitly evidenced value, including an explicitly defined synthetic case.

| Measure | Basis | Prohibited aggregation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restricted and unrestricted cash | Actual receipts/payments, source and settlement currencies, custodian, purpose, gross/fees/net and reversal references; reviewed cash roll-forward. | Never add an unpaid award, credit balance, loaned asset price or service estimate. Keep liability-bearing receipts and different custodians separately classified. |
| Payables, reservations and reimbursements | Actual authorization, obligation, eligible delivery, invoice/expense identity, prior settlement and duplicate check. | A reservation is not an expense payment; reimbursing a purchase is not a second cost or new received support. |
| Noncash entitlement and custody | Type-specific actual units, rights, consumption, expiry, restrictions, ownership and source record. | Never sum heterogeneous units or call a device loan a donated asset. |
| Estimated or realized avoided cost | Same useful eligible scope; evidenced alternative purchase and price/date/currency, actual consumption, incremental charges and stated method. Label forecast versus realized. | Do not multiply a provider maximum/list price by unused entitlement or add the result to cash. Missing counterfactual demand stays Unknown. |
| Obligations and contingent exposure | Agreement/clause, event/period, value or nonfinancial requirement, authorization, actual status and safe evidence. | Unknown fees, return costs or liabilities are not zero and cannot be hidden by a gross “support total.” |

For each fund and currency, reconcile opening cash + confirmed inflows − confirmed outflows = closing cash, explaining differences and keeping commitments separate. Preserve gross and fees even when settlement is net. Apply the accepted #24 currency, refund, duplicate, restricted-purpose and custody rules; qualified accounting/tax decisions remain with the actual authorized custodian and reviewer. A mixed-benefit dashboard should show separate columns, not one invented unrestricted-income figure.

## Obligations, reporting and renewal

Create one stable obligation ID per actual clause/output, linked to the exact agreement and benefit. The [report template](../templates/award-report.md) links rather than replaces the obligation ledger.

| Required field | Recording rule |
| --- | --- |
| Governing clause | Official terms/accepted agreement version, exact clause and applicability to this recipient, benefit and period; preserve conflicting or unavailable evidence. |
| Scope and recipient | Required output, acceptance standard, report period, recipient/channel and permitted disclosure. No confidential text or recipient details in a public copy. |
| Due kind | Absolute date/time, relative interval after a specified event, recurring schedule, event-only requirement, or Unknown with reason. |
| Time evidence | Preserve the published date/time/timezone precision. Keep trigger evidence, period and calendar/business-day definition. If event, timezone or calendar rule is missing, the calculated due instant stays Unknown; do not invent midnight, UTC or a trigger date. |
| Actual responsibility | Covered owner/operator, independent reviewer, accepted scope and fallback/escalation. Unassigned stays visible. |
| Status evidence | Pending, Blocked, Partial, Delivered, Submitted, Acknowledged, Accepted or Closed only within the record's explicit meaning and evidence. A submission acknowledgment does not prove accepted output. |
| Change and closeout | Original obligation, actual amendment/waiver/renewal evidence and scope, remaining obligations and an independently reviewed closeout decision. No silent replacement. |

Prepare reports by comparing the **same planned period and scope** with actual delivered artifacts, versions, methods, counts and results. Retain failures, unexecuted scope and incomplete evidence. Explain changes and link any actual approved deviation; a proposed variance is not an accepted amendment. Separate financial settlement from technical output acceptance.

The independent reviewer records the exact report revision, inputs actually examined, result and remaining gaps. Actual dispatch requires the covered #68 authority and verified recipient/channel. Record attempt, confirmed submission and provider response separately. Do not silently retry an uncertain dispatch.

### Proposed internal monthly review

This cadence is an internal procedure proposal, not a provider deadline, assigned calendar event or installed automation. For a defined period, reconcile each actual benefit/fund, usage and charges; compare open obligations with actual terms; identify expiry, reporting and renewal risks; confirm actual responsible people and valid authorizations; and record the independent review and concrete next action. If no real benefit is established, record that evidence limit rather than a fabricated empty financial ledger.

A renewal needs current program/recipient eligibility, remaining useful need, current terms and costs, continuity/export/return implications and authorization covering the actual renewal. Changed terms, parties, privileges, data use, cost or obligations require the corresponding re-review under #68. An internal reminder does not prevent auto-renewal or overage: verify the actual controls and authorized exit plan before their decision boundary.

### Closeout and termination

For each covered scope, compare actual agreement requirements with settlement, final reports and provider decisions; verify authorized account/license revocation, data export/deletion or retention, asset return/title transfer and any refund or remaining liability. Shipping a loaned asset is not proof of received return; an export request is not verified recoverability. Do not destroy records to create a clean closure.

Record Closed only for the items whose required evidence and independent review are complete. List residual retention, audit, confidentiality, publicity, reporting or repayment obligations separately with their source and owner. If those facts are unknown, overall closeout remains Partial or Blocked. Preserve original awards, uses, failed attempts and reported results; renewal or closure changes no past fact and grants no authority to terminate a real contract.
