## Established scope

The [current program boundary](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/blob/d667319d489b3081d67f88f715b0a5e2f27f6102/README.md#current-boundaries) records the community's **intention to pursue US 501(c)(3) recognition**. This is evidence of the stated intention. It is not evidence of an existing entity, filing, recognition, charitable eligibility, fiscal sponsorship or authority to make a tax-deductibility representation.

The public GitHub namespace and repositories are observed project references. Their account metadata, source licenses and contributor/reviewer identities do not establish the legal owner of every asset or a person's authority to bind that owner. The [accepted program setup](../program-setup-acceptance.md) expressly preserves the charter's organizational adoption and the unfinished legal, financial and applicant questions. The [charter](../charter.md), [structure comparison](structure-decision-brief.md) and [authorization procedure](../workflow-and-authorization.md) remain their own scoped materials.

The concrete public benefit of this work is a reusable, source-linked description of what the project can truthfully represent when comparing support routes. It separates open research and engineering assets from unverified ownership and obligations, so later applications do not confuse a GitHub organization, a project name, a contributor or a proposed host with the actual applicant. No achieved charitable impact, support award, cash value or staffing estimate is inferred.

## Evidence labels and limits

**Observed public reference** means the named page, file or API response was read and supports only its stated scope. **Document-verified fact** requires the appropriate primary administrative record and an actual verification receipt; none of the missing legal/financial facts below has that receipt. **Unknown** means the needed evidence was not established in this inspection, not that no entity, account, agreement or obligation exists. **Not applicable** always includes a reason.

The official IRS pages were opened on **2026-10-19**. The IRS distinguishes state nonprofit status from federal income-tax exemption, identifies organizational documents/state requirements/EIN among preparation steps, and separates preparation from the applicable recognition application. Its EIN guidance says the organization should be legally formed before applying. These sources do not identify Pick Your AI's actual entity, select a state, establish recognition or choose an eligible application form. [G1–G3]

No jurisdiction or verified legal name was supplied by the inspected records, so no name-only registry result is treated as a match or as proof of absence. The connector rejected the organization-profile API endpoint as unsupported; the successful direct repository responses provide the limited namespace/account-type evidence instead. A read-only Git fetch briefly returned HTTP 503 and then succeeded at the stated base. Neither access result is evidence about legal or administrative status. No private account, formation, banking, tax, registrar or contract material was requested or accessed.

## Private-evidence request checklist

This is a prepared checklist for the actual authorized evidence custodian and reviewer; **no request has been sent**. Agree on a verified private route before handling records. Public GitHub issues should contain only the minimum permitted verification receipt and unresolved scope, not the underlying confidential material. No credential, private key, recovery code, full banking detail or personal identity document is needed in the public record.

1. **Identity and status:** obtain the appropriate formation/governing and current-status records. Match exact entity names, jurisdiction and dates. Verify any EIN privately against that entity and any recognition separately; publish only permitted status metadata and a safe evidence reference.
2. **Authority:** identify the actual representative, current capacity, issuer of the mandate, allowed actions, amount/term limits where applicable and any expiry or revocation condition. A public username, role label or repository permission alone is not the mandate. Verify representative and account-control claims through their appropriate evidence independently.
3. **Assets and access:** inventory repository/domain/store/hosting/financial accounts, assets and agreements without recording secrets. For each, distinguish legal owner, administrator, payer and custodian. Verify any proposed transfer against the rights holder and the relevant provider/agreement terms.
4. **Funds and obligations:** privately reconcile dated statements/accounting and agreements to awards, cash receipts, activated credits, restrictions, liabilities, renewals and reporting. Keep unknown periods and incomplete coverage visible; never replace a missing balance with zero.
5. **Fiscal hosting, if claimed:** check the executed agreement and acceptance, exact project/host/applicant identities, custody, authorized representations, fee/exit terms and the particular provider's treatment. A host's general website is not evidence of this project's membership.
6. **Review and disclosure:** have the reviewer record the exact fact checked, evidence version/date, identity match, material conflict, result and limitations. Confirm the permission and minimal scope for publishing that result. Preserve originals in the actual custodian's approved location; do not invent a storage location, custodian or reviewer.
