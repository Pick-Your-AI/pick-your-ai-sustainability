# Sustainability risk register template

Use with the canonical [indicator method](../docs/risk-indicators.md). This is a
blank operating record, not evidence of an actual exposure, assigned person,
accepted threshold or completed action. Use explicit **Unknown** or **Not
applicable — reason** where appropriate. Keep restricted originals in authorized
storage; publish only permitted evidence and summaries.

For each indicator, retain a row even when it cannot be calculated:

| Observation ID | Indicator and scope | Numerator with unit | Denominator with unit | Coverage and unresolved rows | Result state / exact value | Input evidence IDs | Actual interpretation and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not replace Unknown, Not applicable or Invalid input with a zero, a compatible
badge or a favorable color. Do not aggregate currency, credit maxima, cash,
equipment value, engineering hours and resource consumption into a common total.
Show any display rounding separately from the retained exact fraction.

## Evidence ledger

| Evidence ID | Actual claim and affected fields | Source locator / version / hash | Source date / actual inspection date | Evidence grade and conditions | Conflicting or unavailable evidence | Permitted public representation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| To assign | To complete | To complete | Unknown until observed | Primary fact, publisher claim, estimate or Unknown, with scope | Record failures or conflicts literally | Do not disclose protected originals. |

Keep actual contractual dates distinct from source access dates and planned
review horizons. Record historical announcements as historical. Explain how each
quantity was obtained; an estimate needs the task/usage model, inputs, units,
uncertainty and limits. A hash is an input identity, not source authentication,
data completeness, continued validity or a redistribution permission.

## Mandatory topic review

For every topic record **evidenced finding**, **Unknown — missing evidence** or
**Not applicable — reason**, and link the affected observation or risk ID.

| Topic | Questions that the actual evidence must resolve | Finding / evidence / risk ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash and commitments | What was actually received, outstanding or returned, by whom and when? Are grouping and denominator complete? | |
| In-kind reliance and usable capacity | Which accepted essential workload can use each actual benefit under its conditions? Are alternatives independently viable? | |
| Expiry and continuation | Which activated benefits expire in the stated horizon, which have unknown dates and which lack confirmed continuation? | |
| Restrictions and reporting | What may funds/resources be used for? What reports, audits, deliverables or notice duties actually apply? | |
| Sponsor influence and exclusivity | Can a term affect rankings, compatibility, treatment of competitors, independent findings or purchasing freedom? | |
| Publication, attribution and branding | Are there review/veto, delayed publication, benchmark disclosure, logo or attribution conditions? | |
| Privacy, data and intellectual property | What may be submitted, retained, exported, reused or disclosed, to whom, and under whose permission? | |
| Portability and custody | Can the workload/data move? What return, maintenance, shipping, insurance or deletion obligations apply? | |
| Renewal, billing and overage | Are price, cap, alerts, automatic renewal, notice and action authority actually evidenced? | |
| Operational continuity | Which critical operations depend on one person or lack accepted primary responsibility, access or succession? | |
| Organizational independence | Do any combined conditions conflict with the accepted program boundary, including dependence hidden across related providers? | |

Copy this table for each distinct finding. A person may be named only after actual
acceptance of the responsibility; otherwise retain the role and “Unassigned”.

## Publication and handoff

Record the exact accepted source and review, remaining questions, P2 foundation
rationale or actual changed priority decision, and downstream handoff. A specific
program still uses its unique record in `data/opportunities.json`; this register
does not create a second opportunity or mark an application, award, activation or
reporting obligation complete. Link later corrections instead of erasing the
original scope or substituting a synthetic example for actual evidence.
