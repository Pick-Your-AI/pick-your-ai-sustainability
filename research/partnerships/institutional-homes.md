**Governance and resources.** The Handbook describes a lasting community relationship: project trademarks move under Foundation stewardship, day-to-day development uses Foundation-managed repositories/issues, and contributors gain decision roles through participation. Existing copyright owners retain their contributions under licenses; this is separate from name/resource control. They are not a verified allocation of production hosting, GPU or native Apple runners, paid model calls, unlimited storage, or a service-level agreement for this project. It preserves applicable earlier policies for prior submissions; repository-placement provisions are neither a universal manual-approval requirement nor a blanket release exemption. ECA 3.2.0 (March 3, 2026) retains contribution ownership subject to licenses and a separate enduring Specification Grant for defined interface/specification material. ## 2. Apache Software Foundation Incubator

**Type and admission.** Incubation prepares a software community for ASF governance. The official guide starts with an existing cooperating community, a champion and public proposal discussion. Donating a project relinquishes unilateral donor control, and the Cookbook requires necessary existing marks to transfer before graduation. This would need actual community consent and title evidence, not an inferred mandate from a GitHub login. [A2][A4]

ICLA, corporate agreement and software-grant templates provide substantial nonexclusive copyright/patent permissions with authority and third-party conditions. They do not assign every project asset. The reviewed versions are ICLA V2.2, corporate form `v r190612`, and an undated software-grant template. An employer agreement does not replace individual requirements. Signed forms have their own handling; designated public profile fields must not be presumed private. None was completed or submitted. [A5][A6][A7][A8]

Apache-2.0 preserves license, modification and applicable notice duties and does not supply general trademark rights. MIT/X11 is among ASF's accepted third-party categories, but that does not clear every dependency, model, dataset, SDK, media file or distribution. Each selected material still requires the accepted #17 rights docket. [A9][A10][D2]

The August 2026 generative-tooling guidance separates disclosure, originality and sufficient provider output rights. It describes additional conditions for third-party material and limitations of automated scanning, includes documentation/images, and supplies no approved-tool list. It does not admit this project's generated assets or establish human authorship. Its discussion of EU/U.S. law is attributed provider guidance. [A11]

**Operations and publication.** Infra describes Foundation repository migration/creation, mailing lists, issue tracking and websites. Requests, platform support and capacity are specific; no GPU, native runner, production host, SLA or paid-service quota was verified. Projects retain security investigation, fixes and releases with ASF coordination. Sensitive vulnerability reports follow their separate private process. [A13][A15]

Incubating projects report monthly for the first three months, then quarterly unless IPMC requires more. Release approval includes separate community/IPMC votes and incubation labeling. The Release Policy requires binding reviewers to verify signed source, compile and test; existing GitHub CI is not a substitute for those future people and votes. Its published file-size guidance concerns release artifacts, not an inferred CI-storage quota. [A1][A14]

Trademark Policy v1.2 (March 2021) separates software licensing from branding and endorsement. Its restrictions on disparaging mark use coexist with examples of unfavorable nominative comparison. Exact proposed naming and independent publication uses need review; the policy is not evidence of either unrestricted endorsement rights or a blanket prohibition on criticism. [A12]

**Cost and exit.** Sponsorship prices are donations to ASF, not an admission or hosting quote. Undated foundation budget averages cannot support a current savings estimate. Actual project fees, overage, migration and continuing effort are **Unknown**. Retirement can stop builds and close or restrict resources; release archives persist.
