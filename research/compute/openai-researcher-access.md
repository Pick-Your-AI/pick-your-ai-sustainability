## 4. Concrete public-benefit use case

**Proposed question:** Can model-generated explanations preserve an AI-stack evaluator's uncertainty, provenance and outcome distinctions when describing a result to a non-expert? This is a proposed explanation-faithfulness study. It does not ask a model to supply authoritative compatibility rules, vendor facts or rankings.

The research repository owns the [source and evidence policy](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/41). The website's [current-result correction](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-website/pull/303) demonstrates a concrete presentation problem: a previous report or missing evidence must not imply current compatibility. These project artifacts motivate the study; their engineering tests are not empirical evidence of model reliability or human comprehension. Hosting/runtime decisions remain in [leaderboard #25](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-leaderboard/issues/25). [P3–P5]

The following is a reviewable design choice, **not an executed experiment or an accepted application**:

1. Prepare twelve independently checked, synthetic case dossiers, three for each existing `pass`, `fail`, `unknown` and `skipped` outcome. Include stale-report, missing-evidence and contradictory-explanation pitfalls where they are semantically valid; do not manufacture impossible expected outcomes to fill a matrix.
2. Give each dossier two meaning-preserving wording variants. A reviewer freezes the inputs, oracle, licenses and hashes before any call. Publicly readable material still needs adequate reuse rights. Exclude personal information, contributor profiles, private builds and unpublished security findings.
3. If a later study is authorized, make three separately recorded invocations per variant and selected model snapshot: **72 requests per model**, before any explicitly budgeted retries. This small pilot checks case coverage and repeatability; it supplies no statistical power or population-level claim. Model selection, token ceilings and requested credit amount remain unresolved until the [demand model #21](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/21) and [evaluation proposal #49](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/49) can consume them.
4. Score outcome/severity fidelity, unsupported certainty, missing or invented provenance, and adherence to the supplied oracle. Retain refusals, errors and ambiguous outputs as separate observations. An independent reviewer adjudicates qualitative cases; a deterministic label match alone does not establish explanation quality.
5. Publish the permitted case manifest, prompts, versions, invocation parameters, scoring procedure, aggregate results, uncertainty and negative findings. Keep the oracle and evaluation policy outside the model. Human comprehension and accessibility benefit require their own authorized participant study; this pilot does not claim either.

Existing deterministic fixtures and provider-free tests can prepare the corpus. Grant-funded calls would only add observed model behavior after qualification and authorization. The proposal leaves #49's full sponsor-neutral study and native prerequisite intact. No selected model, acquired API account, collected participant evidence or successful benchmark is implied.
