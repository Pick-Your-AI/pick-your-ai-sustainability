## Mechanism matrix

| Mechanism | What could arrive | Classification and release rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restricted grant | Money for a specified eligible purpose, sometimes paid against milestones or subject to reporting | Record a prospective award separately from received restricted cash. The actual executed award determines eligible costs, payment requests, restrictions, return obligations and permitted transfers. |
| Commissioned work | Payment for defined deliverables under a contract | Obtain the actual instrument. A program's use of “investment” does not itself establish ownership, debt or a charitable grant. Scope, acceptance, tax and IP terms control the treatment. |
| Donation channel | Voluntary payments initiated by donors | Channel access is not an award or guaranteed income. Gross receipts, processor/host fees, refunds, chargebacks, restrictions and net settled cash need separate records. |
| Fiscal hosting | Custody, payment and administrative services | Host-held funds are not unrestricted money in the project's account. Host authorization and agreement determine custody, payment eligibility and exit. |
| Maintainer/contributor payment | Money to a named participating person or selected project recipient | Identify the beneficiary and award/payee agreement. Do not redirect a participant's payment into project funds or count the same benefit twice. |
| Loan | Borrowed principal paired with repayment obligations | Principal is financing, not income from a grant. Assess interest, fees, security, guarantees, covenants, repayment and default from an actual offer; no borrowing is proposed here. |
| Equity investment / SAFE | Capital exchanged for current or future ownership rights | Treat dilution, conversion, control, information and liquidity terms as investment obligations. A SAFE is not interchangeable with a convertible note; the YC form describes future equity rather than interest-bearing debt. [P12–P14] |
| Credit, license, equipment or service | Conditional access, capacity, goods or waived fees | Maintain noncash quantity, expiry, restrictions and any actual otherwise necessary avoided cost. A credit cannot settle an unrelated invoice; a hardware “grant” need not be cash or cost-free ownership. |

### Concrete release checklist

1. **Select one evidenced need.** Record the existing repository issue, permitted public-benefit work, expected output and finite acceptance condition. Do not reopen completed technical work as a fundraising justification.
2. **Name the actual legal recipient privately and establish authority.** Resolve the appropriate one of the three routes, host/customer/payee, controlled documents and person who can bind that recipient. Keep Unknown where evidence is absent.
3. **Obtain current primary intake and the exact proposed instrument.** Record provider/version/date, country, call/track, complete relevant annexes and actual payment path. Preserve access failures and contradictory clauses. A sample or FAQ is insufficient for contract acceptance.
4. **Obtain independent legal and financial reviews of the material terms.** The actual proposed signatory must not substitute for the reviewer of their own commitment. Record the review scope, identity, exact document version, findings and dated resolution. Fiscal-host officer approval is additionally required where the actual agreement provides it. An in-session research COMMENT is not a legal opinion or financial appointment.
5. **Prove affordability and record restrictions.** Prepare gross/fee/net calculations by currency, cost allocation, liquidity timing and reservations against known authorized balances. No speculative award, match, credit or personal stipend can fill an otherwise unfunded gap.
6. **Accept only the resolved instrument through the authorized process.** A material change of recipient, amount, scope, timing, rights or liability invalidates the earlier review and returns the proposal to steps 2–5.
7. **Post only actual evidence.** Separate contingent awards, commitments, received unrestricted funds, received restricted funds, held funds, participant payments, debt and noncash support. Reconcile uncertain/duplicate payment status before another attempt, following the financial-controls draft.
8. **Track fulfillment and exit.** Record actual due events and responsible accepted roles; confirm reports, refunds, renewals, exports and rights at completion. Do not auto-renew or manufacture a due date.

A financial acceptance request is blocked if any material contract, authority, amount, source access, cost allocation, rights or liability question remains unresolved. Research on that question may proceed. This is a concrete engineering dependency, not an additional permission request to the user for the already authorized repository work.

## Defensible value and effort method

The only support amount that can be booked as money received is a reconciled actual receipt to the correct recipient. Before that, keep nominal offers or applications in a contingent register. Use the following formulas as a proposed accounting method, with Unknown inputs remaining Unknown.

- **Net settled cash by currency** = reconciled gross receipt minus actual host/processor/withholding/FX deductions and recorded returns. Do not combine currencies without a dated, sourced conversion policy.
- **Available for a specified payment** = known permitted balance minus restricted/unavailable amounts and existing authorized reservations. Eligibility of the payment must be checked independently of the numeric balance.
- **Maximum temporary funding gap** = the largest cumulative eligible outflow before the matching actual cash-in schedule. Use dated milestones and realistic settlement/acceptance conditions; do not fill a missing receipt with a probability-weighted award.
- **Incremental noncash value** = evidenced necessary eligible usage that would otherwise be purchased, minus existing free entitlement and incremental transition/administration/overage costs. Cap usage at actual eligibility, limits and expiry. Never add the same avoided cost to both cash and credit totals.
- **Effort** = separately estimated preparation, specialist/host review, delivery, evidence retention, reporting, renewal and exit tasks. Use actual work breakdown and measured or independently justified time; proposed roles and capacity remain Unassigned.
