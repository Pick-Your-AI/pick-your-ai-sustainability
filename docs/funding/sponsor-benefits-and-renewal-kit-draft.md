## 2. Classify the actual arrangement before choosing a benefit

Use the transaction's substance, parties, restrictions and governing terms. Calling a payment a donation, sponsorship or membership does not establish its treatment. Acknowledgment rights, earned services, restricted grants, platform relationships and equipment loans can carry different obligations. Separate them when necessary; do not bundle an unreviewed commercial promise into a charitable receipt.

| Possible recipient route | What this kit can support | Evidence required before an offer, receipt or activation | Present PYA disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current community without a verified recipient arrangement | Draft ordinary factual thanks and a proposed finite public-work scope. | Actual recipient/custodian, legal capacity, authorized signer/sender, permissible payment route, applicable treatment, source restrictions and independent conflict review. A public repository or account name is not those facts. | Unknown; no claim that the community can issue charitable acknowledgments or bind others. |
| Project under a fiscal sponsor | Draft a host-reviewed request, acknowledgment and reporting packet for the actual accepted project. | Executed relationship, covered project/scope, host policies and fees, delegated mandates, custody, signer/issuer identity, expenditure and notice rules, restricted-fund treatment and exit terms. A possible host's general service page is not an agreement with PYA. | Unknown; no host selected, no application submitted and no host role exercised. |
| Independently recognized 501(c)(3) | Draft issuer-specific charitable or commercial records after the actual arrangement is classified. | Current recognition and correct entity, jurisdiction/year, mandates and accounts; gift versus exchange analysis; restrictions, return benefits, disclosure and record requirements. Recognition alone does not settle every transaction or donor's deduction. | Unknown; no recognition, exemption, deduction or authority is asserted. |

[SPI's services](https://www.spi-inc.org/projects/services/) illustrate why a host route needs its own evidence: services concern associated projects, expense approval and contract signing have different functions, and custody/transfer conditions matter. The page gives no PYA relationship or mandate. Do not copy a potential host's officers, policy amounts, fees or approval path into an actual PYA record without verification.

Before a proposed benefit enters review, record the agreement or proposal ID, exact parties and recipient route, benefit ID/model, cash versus noncash/loan/service classification, restrictions, relationship conflicts and the canonical work owner. If two sources conflict, preserve both and the unresolved question. The default is **withhold the affected action** until a qualified interpretation and actual authority are recorded, not to choose the more convenient term.

## 4. Cost, capacity and public-benefit worksheet

Use one worksheet for each proposed option and agreement version. A reviewer should be able to trace every entered amount or effort unit to actual evidence and distinguish a planning assumption from an observation.

| Field | Required entry and refusal condition |
| --- | --- |
| Work unit and quantity | Exact task, deliverable, scope, term, quantity and acceptance evidence. Unknown quantity means total effort is Unknown. |
| Role capacity | Necessary functions, actual authorized holder/mandate evidence in a suitable private record, competence, remaining capacity and conflicts. A role label or issue assignee is not available capacity. |
| Effort | Preparation + quantity × per-unit work + independent review + maintenance + reporting + closeout. Identify and deduplicate shared work instead of billing it twice. Keep assumptions/ranges distinct from observed effort. |
| Delivery cost | Verified direct cash items/currency and a separately supported labor-cost basis. Unpaid or unpriced labor is Unknown, not zero. Identify restricted eligibility and source of each quote. |
| Financial quantities | Keep proposed price, voluntary support amount, settled funds, outstanding pledge, gross receipts, fees, tax, restricted available balance, economic delivery cost and return-benefit fair market value in distinct fields. |
| Exposure and reserve | Actual approved cap, term, consumption limit, renewal/overage rule and exit reserve. A cap that cannot be enforced before metered exposure does not qualify activation. |
| Noncash value | Native units, eligible useful workload, actual consumption and otherwise-required purchasing evidence; deduct associated cost and avoid double counting. No advertised maximum becomes cash or automatic savings. |
| Evidence status | Source/version/date, assumptions, scope of review and unresolved conflict for every value. Missing evidence remains Unknown; no arbitrary midpoint or zero fills it. |

A conditional cost-coverage calculation can aid review where every variable fee is evidenced to apply to the same gross amount: gross coverage = (fixed eligible cost + approved reserve) / (1 − total same-base fee rate), only when that total is below one. If fees use different bases, minimums, caps, timing or taxes, calculate the actual terms separately. This is algebra for a stated scenario; it establishes no donor demand, price, funding target, spendable balance or return-benefit value. No actual numerical amount is proposed here.

Use the [financial control record](../../templates/financial-control-record.md) for approval, receipt, restriction and reconciliation evidence. Requester, independent approver, signer, payment operator and reconciler remain distinct functions where the received control method requires them. If separation cannot be met, record that gap and use only an actually approved compensating arrangement; the kit does not grant one.

### Reporting useful public outcomes

A concrete initial use is to describe an independently accepted public documentation/accessibility improvement, an evidence correction or a bounded reproducibility result from its actual source. B03/B04 can link that public work and the real receiving decision, while reporting missing measurements honestly. This does not duplicate the research, application, native-device or participant-study implementation.

Apply the [public-benefit observation template](../../templates/public-benefit-observation.md) and its PB-01–PB-07 methods. Record the exact construct, source/revision, observation window, method, unit, numerator/denominator, missingness, uncertainty and review scope. Explain whether a statement is an engineering output, a human observation or a financial quantity. Do not combine unlike units into a single impact score.

A merged PR, passing test or published note can be an evidenced output. It does not establish how many people benefited, a unique human count, reduced risk, adoption, causation, a donor's financial return or achieved target. A source account is not necessarily a distinct person. The unresolved customer/participant methods and rights remain with their existing owners. Baselines and outcome values without observations stay Unknown; prospective targets stay labeled prospective.

Reports include adverse, incomplete and null outcomes under the same agreed method. Sponsor review may correct factual relationship details through the normal independent process; it cannot change findings. Use the same public version for everyone unless a separately justified and authorized access rule applies to some unrelated material. Funding creates no right to private evidence.

## 5. A packet must pass review before any external action

Create the packet as a versioned review artifact using the existing [outreach draft](../../templates/outreach-draft.md), [award report](../../templates/award-report.md), neutrality and financial templates where applicable. Keep the public method separate from an authorized private system containing actual donor/contact, contract, bank, tax and account details.

A minimally complete packet identifies:

1. Exact proposal/agreement and version, parties, route, model/benefit IDs, classification and source of authority.
2. Specific work and recipient/channel; actual capacity, cost, restrictions, dates/term and exit basis, with unresolved fields marked.
3. Correct acknowledgment variant and each factual claim's evidence; permissions for names, marks, assets and public statements.
4. Applicable primary terms and unresolved conflicts, including actual platform/host agreements and signer/issuer requirements.
5. Independently recorded findings review and the separate operational approvals required by the actual arrangement.
6. Exact approved content/version, recipient and channel for any send, publish, account or financial action.
7. After an authorized action, its actual outcome, timestamps and permitted record location; an unsent draft or failed action stays labeled that way.

Research acceptance and a merged document do not supply item 6. Do not send a message, publish a sponsor identity/logo, open an account, click a tier, apply to a host, promise work, receive/transfer funds or issue a tax statement solely because this kit was accepted.

Store only approved public-safe conclusions in GitHub. Do not put donor identities, bank/card details, tax identifiers, private contacts, confidential agreements or participant records into example fields. A public URL or existing public fragment does not establish permission for a new disclosure. Record retention/deletion and access rules must come from an actual authorized arrangement; this draft sets no universal retention period.

Portability is a reviewed export of permitted records, with readable identity, version, restrictions and access controls. It is not an automatic transfer of a host-held asset, donor consent, payment subscription, signature or licence. On export, distinguish factual public history from private records and current authority, and preserve enough permitted linkage to reconcile the original arrangement.

### Checklist for an actual arrangement

**Before activation:** resolve the route, classification, conflict, governing terms, restrictions, signature/issuer/custody, capacity/cost, rights/privacy and enforceable exposure/exit limits. Confirm the acknowledgment/report options are actually permitted. No checkbox in this proposed kit supplies the evidence.

**Before each report:** read the applicable term, verify the reporting period and evidence, reconcile scope and permitted financial statements, label missing outcomes, review rights/privacy and approve the exact publication. Keep report production separate from sending or publishing it.

**Before renewal or a new-price offer:** inspect the current agreement and all remaining commitments, actual service/use/funding records, permitted benefit performance, updated capacity, eligibility, cost, conflict and terms. Compare a finite new proposal with ending support. A prior favorable research assessment is not ongoing applicant qualification.

**Before overage or extension:** identify the applicable meter/cap and decision authority, available eligible funding, full downstream cost and safe stopping point. No expectation of a renewal, credit, reimbursement or later approval can authorize current exposure. If a promised limit cannot be enforced, withhold activation or additional use.

**Before termination/expiry:** determine the actual party/notice rights and effective event; preserve restrictions, financial reconciliation, return of loaned resources, mark removal, data/access disposition and any surviving commitments. Do not equate removing a web listing with cancelling a payment relationship, or cancelling a platform relationship with satisfying a separate agreement.

**At closeout:** record actual accepted work, incomplete obligations, permitted public reporting, reconciled restricted/unrestricted/noncash records and authorized returns or changes. Preserve the historical relationship accurately. Do not invent a refund, release, transfer or universal retention period.

### Platform and host-specific boundaries

The [GitHub organization setup guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-organization) describes published tier prices as immutable: a new price uses a new tier, while retiring the old tier leaves existing participants on it until their relationship changes, ends or expires. A tier change therefore needs an explicit existing-obligations check. This kit creates no tier or account action.

The [Sponsors Additional Terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms) distinguish sponsored developers from sponsoring parties and place obligations on the relevant party. Their offer/content/support/refund and termination provisions must be read for the actual relationship. Do not copy a sponsor-party anniversary or notice condition into a developer's benefit agreement. Actual platform, host and benefit contracts can be distinct; unresolved conflicts require review before action.

Host approval, platform permission and independent project acceptance are different authorities. One cannot substitute for another. A record export must not imply transfer of a host-held asset or a payment relationship, and a contribution may not be portable merely because its public evidence link is.
