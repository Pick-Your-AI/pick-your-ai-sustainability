# Financial controls and restricted-fund procedure

Owning issue: [E02-06 / #24](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/24). Prepared **2026-10-19 UTC** for independent review. This is a proposed operating procedure, not evidence of an adopted budget, appointed officer, account, payment or gift.

**Recommendation:** accept this P1 foundational control design after independent review, then use it with the actual entity or fiscal host's accepted authority and terms for a concrete transaction. Research acceptance can be complete while the corresponding financial action remains blocked. The procedure does not establish Pick Your AI's tax status or a donor's entitlement to a deduction.

## Basis and three operating routes

This procedure consumes the [administrative evidence baseline](../nonprofit/entity-baseline.md) at accepted repository revision `52c439c056beed504ee1d0ec2e904213f507e456`. Its actual legal entity, tax classification, sponsor relationship, financial balances, account custody and signatory mandates remain Unknown. Unknown balances are never entered as zero. GitHub permissions and project engineering authorization do not establish those administrative facts.

The existing [action-authorization procedure](../workflow-and-authorization.md), [approval record](../../templates/application-approval.md) and [activation/reporting procedure](../operations-and-reporting.md) retain their own #68/#69 acceptance. This document supplies their #24 financial-control input without declaring those issues complete. The [record template](../../templates/financial-control-record.md) and [worked examples](../../templates/examples/financial-controls-worked-example.md) make the controls reviewable.

| Route | Required operating basis | Current disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present community or individual contributor | Identify the actual contracting party, account owner, mandate, permitted activity and applicable reporting. An individual's purchase is not automatically a project obligation or reimbursable expense. | Actual facts Unknown; retain a proposed record without spending or claiming charitable status. |
| Project through a fiscal sponsor or host | Verify the executed hosting agreement, the host's precise classification, fund custody, authorized contacts, expense process, signer, fees and exit restrictions. Apply that host's rules to the particular transaction. | No actual host relationship established by this procedure. SPI and OSC examples below are different published arrangements. |
| Independently recognized 501(c)(3) | Verify the actual entity, recognition/classification, governing mandate, restricted-purpose terms, accounting policies and authorized financial actors. Recognition alone does not approve an expense. | Actual recognition and appointments Unknown; no application, banking or receipt authority inferred. |

The concrete public benefit is a traceable basis for project infrastructure, research, accessibility and distribution support across the six repositories. These controls help avoid unsupported commitments, duplicate reimbursement and inflated support totals. They are not a funding program: application process, provider deadline and award amount are **Not applicable** to this procedure. A real program's eligibility, intake, deadlines and restrictions remain in its existing opportunity record.

## Roles and segregation of duties

All actual role holders are **Unassigned** until supported by an accepted mandate. A reviewer of this research is not thereby appointed to any financial role.

| Function | Responsibility | Required separation and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requester / potential payee | Explain purpose, amount, currency, evidence, restrictions and conflicts; identify prior requests and reimbursements. | Cannot approve their own benefit, reimbursement or related-party request under this proposed control. |
| Budget/commitment approver | Verify purpose, available eligible funds, maximum total exposure, restrictions and the precise scope before an obligation is made. | Must be independent of the requester/payee and have actual authority over that fund and commitment. |
| Signer / host representative | Review and execute the applicable agreement within the actual mandate. | Repository access, a project liaison role or expense approval does not substitute for signing authority. SPI's H1 rule reserves contracting to its officer unless its board authorizes an exception. |
| Payment operator / custodian | Verify an approved payable, correct payee and account, payment limits and required host controls; record the actual result. | No self-release of the operator's own claim. Approval is not proof of payment. Hosted money remains under host custody. |
| Reconciler / independent reviewer | Match the evidence trail, statements, fund balances, exceptions and reports; check changed or duplicate claims. | Independent of the transaction's requester and payment execution; record actual capacity and conflicts. |

Separate functions do not automatically require four new appointees. Any permitted combination must be documented under the actual governing or host mandate while retaining the independent checks above. If adequate independent authority is unavailable, keep the action blocked. A host's actual documented review may supply a required control only within its verified scope; a queue label or assumed future review cannot.

## Record and ledger model

Use one stable control-record ID linked to the existing provider/program and, when known, round and agreement. Record amendments and payment attempts as linked events. The ledger is an operational control register; the actual custodian's qualified accounting treatment remains separate.

Maintain these distinct ledgers or explicit account classes in the authorized accounting system. This is not an instruction to open bank accounts or move funds.

| Ledger | Record | Exclude from this total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restricted cash | Actual received cash by currency, custodian, restricted-purpose/fund ID, permitted costs, fees, disbursements and reversals. | Unreceived awards, unauthorized transfers between purposes, and in-kind face value. |
| Unrestricted cash | Actual cash without an established donor/contract restriction, with its evidence and custody. Unknown restriction status remains unresolved pending review. | Credits, property estimates, services or cash held for a different legal party. |
| In-kind benefits | Credits, licenses, contributed services, donated property and loans as distinct types; units, rights, availability, consumption, custody and limits. | Automatic cash-equivalent or unrestricted-income totals. A loan is not donated ownership. |
| Commitments, payables and reimbursement claims | Prior authorization, actual obligation, delivered work, invoice/receipt, outstanding payable, prior payment and settlement references. | A second expense for a previously paid invoice or a second cost entry when reimbursing the same underlying purchase. |

Every cash event retains the original amount/currency, gross receipt or charge, each actual fee, net settlement, booking date, evidence date and transaction reference. Reconcile gross minus documented fees to net; do not assume every fee has the same basis. Preserve refunds, credit notes, reversals and chargebacks as linked entries rather than deleting the original. Restore the affected restricted balance only when the returned funds and continuing restriction are established.

Never add different currencies without an explicit reporting conversion. Preserve source and settlement currencies, actual applied exchange rate, rate source/date, conversion fees, rounding policy and the resulting amount. An estimate stays labeled as an estimate; an unknown settlement does not become a guessed rate or zero fee. Host-held and independently held balances must not be presented as one freely transferable pool. SPI's published H1 transfer restrictions are an example of why custody and exit terms matter.

For credits and software, retain awarded face value, actually available entitlement, consumed eligible units, cash charges and realized avoided cost separately. Avoided cost requires an evidenced counterfactual purchase for the same useful scope; it is not automatically the provider's list price. Record hardware donation versus loan, owner, custodian, condition, return terms and disposition permission. Donor acknowledgment valuation and internal asset/accounting valuation are separate decisions.

## Prior approval through payment

1. **Describe the proposed obligation.** Link the exact quote, program, applicant, fund, public-benefit output, amount/currency, term, taxes, fees, recurring costs and possible overage. Mark incomplete inputs Unknown. Record whether a contract, purchase, reimbursement or completed-work invoice is proposed.
2. **Check eligibility and restrictions.** Confirm actual entity/host authority, allowable purpose, rights, privacy, reporting, branding, publication, portability, renewal, termination and independence. Identify which actual clauses impose conditions. A technical budget alert is not proof that a provider prevents overage.
3. **Reserve a supported maximum.** Compare proposed exposure with reconciled eligible funds and existing commitments. Record the available amount, reservation and budget owner. Unknown balances or uncapped/unapproved exposure block commitment; an award notice is not spendable cash. A reservation is not a cash payment.
4. **Obtain independent, exact approval before obligation.** Link the accepted record with approver/capacity, artifact revision, fund, purpose, amount/currency cap, term and validity conditions. Then obtain any distinct signer or host approval. Do not promise reimbursement for an unauthorized prior purchase or assume retroactive approval. Document an exception request without presenting it as accepted.
5. **Verify the payable.** Match delivered work or goods to the approved scope. For an invoice, inspect the specific work and performance period. For reimbursement, inspect the actual purchase/payment evidence and prior approvals. Confirm payee identity privately under the authorized process. Resolve tax forms and payment prerequisites with the actual custodian.
6. **Search for duplicates before submission and again before payment.** Compare vendor/payee, invoice or receipt identity, dates, amounts/currencies, line items, project allocations and earlier payment/refund references across relevant funds and hosts. A new upload name or record ID does not make the same expense new. Partial allocations must sum to no more than the supported eligible amount, net of prior reimbursement and refunds.
7. **Release and record only the approved payable.** The authorized operator verifies that approval, restrictions and payee details still match. Preserve submission, processing, failure, uncertainty and confirmed settlement separately. After an ambiguous payment result, reconcile with the custodian before another attempt; do not infer that silence means unpaid.
8. **Reconcile and report.** Match the actual statement/host entry and receipt to the payable and restriction ledger; release or adjust the reservation without double counting the expense. Have the independent reviewer record the exact scope, result, exceptions and remaining obligations.

A changed applicant, payee, account, amount/currency, purpose, deliverable, material term, data handling, restriction or renewal invalidates reliance on the previous approval for that changed scope. Retain the old record and obtain a new decision. Prior approval for one provider or period does not approve another. Actual payment instructions and credentials remain in the custodian's authorized private system.

## Donation acknowledgments and return benefits

First establish the actual recipient and its authority to issue the particular acknowledgment. A platform confirmation, donation label or proposed nonprofit intent is not an adopted charitable-tax representation. Hosted receipts follow the actual host; OSC's stated 501(c)(6) treatment must not be replaced with SPI or 501(c)(3) language. Have qualified finance/tax review resolve a concrete recipient, gift type, jurisdiction and reporting year before issuing applicable tax statements. [F1, F2, H8]

For an applicable charitable acknowledgment, F1 specifies the recipient organization, cash amount, a description **without a donor-gift value** for noncash property, and the appropriate statement about goods or services supplied in return. When return benefits exist, include their description and supported good-faith value; use the no-return-benefit statement only when true. Record the actual gift date and receipt identity in the control trail. Do not include sensitive tax identifiers in a public record. [F1]

The donor's contemporaneous acknowledgment requirement applies to a single contribution of **$250 or more**. Contemporaneous receipt is tied to the earlier of the donor's filing date and applicable return due date, including extensions; no unobserved project deadline is invented. Exceptions need their applicable current basis. The publication's 2023 inflation examples are not current-year defaults. Donated services and unreimbursed expenses have distinct treatment, so do not issue a deductible-hours valuation. [F2]

Separately, for an applicable quid pro quo payment **over $75**, the recipient's written disclosure must explain that the deductible portion is limited to the contribution exceeding the fair market value of the return benefits and provide a good-faith estimate of that value. The threshold concerns the **total payment**, even if the potentially deductible portion is $75 or less. Furnish this disclosure with the **solicitation or receipt**; a disclosure already supplied for the particular solicitation need not be duplicated at receipt. Record the actual content, delivery event and applicable exception, if any. This timing is distinct from the donor's contemporaneous acknowledgment deadline. [F3]

Use the record template to prepare a **draft** acknowledgment decision, including unresolved return benefits, authorized issuer, private delivery channel and review. This procedure neither sends a receipt nor determines a donor's deduction. Loaned hardware remains a custody record, not a description of donated ownership.

## Monthly reconciliation, obligations and closeout

For each actually held fund, close a defined period against the authoritative custodian records. The monthly cadence is this proposed internal control, not a provider deadline or an installed automation.

The reconciler checks opening balance plus actual inflows less actual outflows against closing balance for each currency and fund; separately matches outstanding commitments, payables, restricted use, refunds and in-kind units. Explain every difference and missing period. Compare approvals with actual charges, recurring subscriptions and usage. The reviewer signs only the scope actually examined. Unreconciled differences remain open; no balancing plug or unsupported zero is inserted.

Create reporting and renewal entries from the actual agreement's clause: required output, period, due date/timezone or event trigger, recipient, acceptance evidence, reporting owner and independent reviewer. Unknown dates and unfilled roles remain explicit. Use the [award report](../../templates/award-report.md) to compare planned and delivered scope, preserve partial results and explain variances. No financial or provider report is submitted merely by preparing it.

Before credit expiry or renewal, verify remaining need, covered use, quotas, enforceable spending limits, data/export rights and new authority. Record who can stop spend and what happens if a technical cap cannot be verified. At exit, reconcile funds and unused benefits, settle or disclose obligations, terminate only under actual authority, preserve required records and arrange authorized return or transfer of assets. The actual terms determine retention, refund, return and renewal dates; no universal term is invented.

## Public and private records

The public record contains only reviewed, permitted project facts: a control ID, purpose, relevant public deliverable, state, permitted aggregate/category information and a safe reference to the independent decision. Public permission for a name, amount, contract or grant is checked separately. Default to the minimum needed; an operational record need not expose every field publicly.

Private banking/payment details, donor identities and contacts, tax forms/identifiers, unredacted receipts, personal addresses, confidential terms, account credentials and private approval evidence stay in the actual custodian's authorized system. Do not invent that system or its custodian. For hosted funds, follow the host's access rules; H6 specifically limits copying or sharing sensitive community information. Reference reviewed private evidence by an opaque internal record ID without publishing access-bearing URLs or hashes of sensitive values. The public and private template sections are a separation design, not a new repository for private records.

## Value, priority and remaining decisions

P1 follows #24's foundational priority: authorization and benefit operations need reliable control inputs before commitments. No urgency, award probability or monetary score is inferred. This is operating work rather than a scored funding opportunity, so no duplicate provider record or invented award is added to `data/opportunities.json`.

Estimate effort only from an actual volume of records, transactions, currencies, agreements and review tasks with observed or explicitly justified time assumptions. Net usable support requires eligible demand, actual benefits, fees, uncovered costs, renewal/exit costs and effort. Cash, noncash entitlement and avoided cost stay separate; no numerical value or staffing commitment is established for this procedure.

The remaining actual operating decisions are entity/host selection, applicable agreements and law, authorized roles, custody and private system, reconciled balances and historical obligations, spending limits, and concrete reporting/retention rules. These block only the actions requiring them. The procedure and synthetic examples can be reviewed without manufacturing their answers.
