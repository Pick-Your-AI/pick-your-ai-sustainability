It is not a product test, actual audit, recruited sample, empirical study, user benefit, donation or findings acceptance. No person, model, participant record, private data or real execution appears here. Every actual project baseline remains **Unknown**.

## A. A traceability audit with a duplicate

An invented card is declared to contain eight distinct claims, C1–C8. A ninth display row repeats C1. The declared PB-03 audit covers all eight distinct claims; the duplicate display does not create another unit. In this fictional example, six claims satisfy all rubric checks, C7 has unavailable supporting evidence, and C8 lacks an explicit reuse status. These two remain audit failures; they are not excluded to improve the rate.

| Field | Synthetic value and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible unit and denominator | Eight unique audited claims; N = 8. This is not eight people. |
| Numerator | Six claims meet every declared check; k = 6. |
| Coverage | Eight of eight unique claims audited. Coverage is separate from success. |
| Traceability result | 6/8 = 75%; the proposed 100% criterion is not met in this illustration. |
| Duplicate handling | The repeated C1 adds no denominator or numerator. |
| Corrective action | Keep the two limitations visible and refer the two distinct findings for correction; no actual issue or source record is fabricated. |
| Independent acceptance | None. Reviewing this example's arithmetic would accept only the example, not a real card or source. |

The nine display rows, eight unique claims, six successes and two failures reconcile. Reporting 6/6 after dropping C7/C8 would violate PB-03. Reporting 7/9 by counting the duplicate would change the unit and also violate the declared method.

## B. Unknown, zero, assistance and withdrawal are different

These are independent hypothetical calculations, not different phases of one actual study and not a proposed recruitment quota.

| Case | Correct treatment |
| --- | --- |

It would not infer two contributions, two people, a beneficiary or downstream adoption from two PR numbers. This example supplies none of those actual receipts.
