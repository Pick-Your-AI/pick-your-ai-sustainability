## Valuation rules

For a support request, calculate expected usage × applicable rate only after confirming unit, currency, conditions, region, availability and expiry. Separate cash, committed cash, credit face value, activated capacity and realized avoided cost. An avoided cost requires evidence that the eligible resource would otherwise have been purchased for the accepted use.

Budget shipping, taxes, overage, migration, maintenance, returns, reporting and post-credit continuation where applicable. Leave unknown values null. Loans, reimbursements, restricted awards and investments have different treatment and require their own assessment.

E02.01 supplies current invoices and inventory; E02.02 produces the accepted full budget; E02.03 supplies reproducible workload demand; E02.06 establishes controls. The 12-month start date and any financial commitment remain undecided.
