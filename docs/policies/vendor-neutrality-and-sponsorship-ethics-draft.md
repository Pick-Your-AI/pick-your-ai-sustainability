## 2. Independence that support cannot purchase

A sponsor must not purchase favorable rankings, benchmark outcomes, research conclusions, compatibility results, deletion or concealment of supported adverse findings, exclusion of competitors, procurement preference or control of repository decisions. This applies to indirect arrangements through affiliates, free credits, personal gifts or conditions on renewal as well as direct cash.

Funding and commissions must not enter organic scoring, evidence admission, compatibility rules, eligibility for comparison or publication decisions. A provider may submit evidence and challenge factual errors through the same documented process available to other participants. Record the evidence and the independent correction decision. A sponsor gets no unpublished veto or privileged opportunity to rewrite a conclusion.

A study may truthfully report that a resource limitation prevents testing a model. It must identify the selection basis and missing coverage. Donated capacity does not make an untested competitor inferior, make selected tests representative, or justify presenting sponsor-selected cases as comprehensive. The study owner must record its evaluated versions, protocol, inclusion/exclusion reasons, material support and limitations in the canonical research record.

When sponsor resources are necessary for reproduction, assess an independent reproducibility or exit path before promising a public outcome. If feasible publication or independent review is incompatible with an offered restriction, revise or decline the proposed arrangement. Preserve the refusal and reason; do not manufacture a passing result to retain support.

## 4. Conflict disclosure, recusal and independent decision

A material interest can include employment, ownership, compensation, family or business relationships, a proposed paid engagement, personal gifts, referral income or influence over a related organization. A researcher, reviewer or decision-maker must disclose an interest relevant to the particular decision before participating in it, and update that disclosure when it changes. A missing declaration is an unresolved check, not evidence of no conflict.

Use this process for the affected arrangement, transaction, research decision or correction:

1. **Open a stable record.** Identify the proposed action and exact source/agreement revision, affected public-benefit need, parties, benefit and existing authorization. Search for related records and amendments so a new ID does not conceal the same arrangement.
2. **Declare the relevant interest privately where necessary.** Record the nature, affected decisions and evidence in the actual authorized custody system. Prepare a permitted public summary of the material relationship. Do not put personal financial details, private donor identities or confidential agreements in GitHub.
3. **Determine the affected scope independently.** An actual reviewer without that material interest identifies whether it could affect the recommendation, selection, approval, payment or publication. Record the reviewer, declared interests, evidence scope and conclusion. A title, automated check or unsupplied future review is insufficient.
4. **Record recusal.** The conflicted person must not select their replacement reviewer, vote on or approve their own benefit, control the affected evaluation, or release their own payment. They may supply requested factual information; record that participation and its limits. Preserve unrelated authorized work that does not create the conflict.
5. **Assign the decision through an actual mandate.** Identify the disinterested replacement and the actual governing/host basis for that person's capacity, quorum and authority. Where those facts are missing, keep the affected action proposed or blocked. Do not invent a board, quorum, appointment or waiver. A research review does not make its reviewer an officer.
6. **Compare terms and alternatives.** The replacement reviewer checks the need, reasonable alternatives, benefit/cost basis, independence, rights, disclosure and full obligations. A related-party offer must not be accepted just because it is free or convenient. Record the disposition: proposed acceptable terms, revise, decline, or unresolved, with reasons.
7. **Approve only through the applicable existing process.** The authorized decision-maker binds any actual decision to the precise parties, artifact, limits, period and continuing conditions under #68 and #24. Preserve separate legal, research, financial and execution scopes. Reuse valid prior authorization instead of asking for the same decision again.
8. **Record the real outcome and follow-up.** Publish only a cleared summary and safe evidence reference. Record actual changes, conditions and execution separately. Recheck the conflict and terms at the next affected decision or material change; an earlier declaration is not a permanent clearance.

A reviewer must have access to the evidence needed for the conclusion they claim. If confidentiality prevents adequate independent review or truthful material disclosure, the unresolved condition prevents acceptance of that arrangement. No anonymous public summary can substitute for a missing private independent review.

An exception request must identify the exact proposed deviation, reason, mandate and safeguards and remain unresolved until decided by competent actual authority. An ordinary transaction exception cannot waive the prohibitions on purchased outcomes, suppressed adverse findings or competitor exclusion. Any proposed change to those core clauses is a policy amendment requiring its own open review and adoption decision, not a sponsor negotiation.

## 5. Clause-level intake, restrictions and exit

The review record must preserve the actual clause/reference, applicable version/date, finding, unresolved fact, action and exit condition for each row. “Not applicable” needs a reason specific to that arrangement; absent terms remain Unknown. Assess the full offer, linked terms and amendments, including click-through conditions.

| Topic | Evidence and proposed decision rule |
| --- | --- |
| Permitted use and restrictions | Match the exact resource, beneficiary, geography and use to a canonical need. Reject requirements to misstate eligibility, evade terms or divert restricted support to an unrelated purpose. |
| Reporting and deliverables | Identify the actual recipient, output, cadence or event trigger, due-date precision, allowed evidence and feasible owner role. Publish only supported outcomes. A report must allow truthful failure, partial completion and limitations. |
| Branding and naming | Check name/logo license, placement, duration, removal and permitted language. A badge must not imply that a sponsor approved an evaluation or that the project endorses a provider. Naming rights and joint marketing need separate explicit scope. |
| Publication, benchmarks and IP | Identify rights in methods, code, data and results; factual/security/confidentiality review, if any, must have a bounded purpose and feasible end. No sponsor veto, outcome-conditioned publication, indefinite delay or transfer of unrelated rights. Preserve lawful privacy/security handling and existing source-use limits. |
| Confidentiality and privacy | Identify the data categories actually needed, parties with access, retention, processing/transfer and lawful disclosure constraints. No donor access to private users, prompts, account data or unpublished security reports as a sponsorship benefit. |
| Portability and operational control | Identify actual owner/admin/custodian rights, export formats, source/artifact availability, migration dependencies and a feasible continuation or shutdown path. Do not promise transfer rights the provider does not grant. |
| Renewal and expiry | Record actual term, deadline/timezone or event trigger, notice period, residual data/benefit treatment and changed terms. Revalidate continuing need and authority. A calendar reminder is not an approved renewal. |
| Overage, fees and exposure | Record charges, taxes, match obligations and maximum supported exposure by currency. Verify actual enforcement and who can stop use; an alert, zero cash entry price or credit balance is not a hard spending cap. |
| Exclusivity and independence | Examine provider, category, placement, procurement and publication restrictions separately. Decline any requirement to exclude a competitor from fair comparison or purchase favorable treatment. A sponsor category label alone establishes no permissible exception. |
| Restricted funds, refunds and returns | Apply the existing financial controls. Preserve the original event and linked reversal; establish custody, permitted purpose, settlement and actual return authority before moving money or property. Do not unilaterally refund, cancel or erase an obligation. |
| Nonperformance or influence demand | Record the actual request/failure and affected obligation, preserve evidence and pause only the disputed new action. Seek a compliant amendment, decline the unaccepted offer, or follow an authorized contractual exit. Preserve truthful published findings and existing legal duties. |

For an unexpected post-acceptance conflict, do not assert that the agreement never happened. The actual operator and authorized decision-maker must reconcile the obligation and available remedy. Any corrective message, cancellation, refund or new commitment needs the authority appropriate to that next action; existing valid scope continues to apply.

## 6. Public disclosure and protected evidence

Publish an understandable material-relationship disclosure with the affected study, recommendation or support acknowledgment, at a location and in a form suitable for its audience. A generic sponsors page does not automatically explain the relationship affecting a particular recommendation. The FTC's guidance is contextual and emphasizes clear disclosure of unexpected material relationships; its affiliate discussion also addresses proximity to the recommendation/link. It is not a universal safe harbor or a determination of this project's legal liability. [S4]

The proposed public record should identify the permitted support class and status, affected work, material restrictions, reviewer/conflict disposition, relevant time period and a safe reference. Use an actual cleared sponsor identity or a sufficient authorized description. Do not promise anonymity while claiming a disclosure adequately explains a material provider relationship when it does not.

Keep private donor identities/contact details, banking and tax information, private agreements, personal interests, credentials, security findings and private mandates in the verified custodian's authorized system. Do not invent that system or its custodian. Use a cleared opaque reference; publishing hashes of sensitive values or access-bearing private links is not a substitute for protecting the evidence.

Permission to disclose a name, amount, logo or agreement is a separate recorded fact. Aggregate information must also be checked for indirect identification. If necessary material facts cannot be disclosed safely and accurately, revise or decline the proposed public benefit instead of presenting an incomplete disclosure as complete.

Do not promise sponsor access to user data or private evaluation material. Legitimate source, privacy and coordinated-security restrictions remain applicable. A sponsor's reputational discomfort is not a security exception or a reason to suppress a supported result.

- Does the verified legal route support the intended public purposes, and how should private benefit, related-party transactions and compensation be assessed for that entity and jurisdiction? Use S1 as a source question, not a status finding.

- What happens to existing agreements, restricted balances, acknowledgments and published research at adoption, amendment or withdrawal? Review actual obligations; do not retroactively assert that this draft governed past work.
