# Sponsorship neutrality — synthetic worked cases

**All parties, amounts, events and proposed decisions below are fictional.** These are causal walkthroughs of the [draft policy](../../docs/policies/vendor-neutrality-and-sponsorship-ethics-draft.md) and [review template](../sponsorship-neutrality-review.md). No real provider register row, award, mandate, receipt, legal conclusion or transaction is created.

## Case A: factual acknowledgment with incomplete operating authority

A fictional supporter offers **USD 500 once** toward a reproducible public testing guide. Its proposed return is its permitted name on a support page. The draft says that partial or unfavorable findings may be published and that the supporter cannot select outcomes.

Record USD 500 as **offered cash**, with actual receipt and restrictions not yet verified. Do not report an award, cash balance, tax deduction or realized avoided cost. Compare the guide with an existing documented need; its delivery effort remains Unknown until the scope and staffing assumptions are evidenced.

The reviewer may find the proposed terms compatible with the draft's independence clauses, subject to exact source, disclosure and rights review. That source finding does not authorize acceptance. The actual recipient, signer, name permission, restrictions and necessary role coverage remain Unknown. The action remains proposed until an existing valid concrete authorization and those facts cover it.

A possible acknowledgment stays an **unsent draft**: “Support for the public testing guide: [permitted supporter name], [verified support class and actual status]. The supporter has no control over methods, inclusion or findings.” Its placeholders are not verified facts. It is not a charitable receipt or an endorsement.

**Exit condition:** establish the actual terms and authority and approve the exact permitted statement, revise the terms, or decline the unaccepted offer. Do not repeatedly request already supplied valid authority.

## Case B: credit face value and a publication veto

A fictional provider advertises **USD 10,000 in credits**, conditioned on excluding a competitor from the comparison and allowing the provider to suppress an unfavorable result. No workload, covered service units, expiry or overage enforcement is verified.

Record an advertised in-kind offer, not USD 10,000 cash or avoided spending. The workload and all cost inputs remain Unknown; no multiplication by a list price is defensible.

The competitor exclusion and outcome veto directly fail the proposed core clauses. Disclosure of the arrangement does not cure those conditions. Recommend **revise or decline**: remove the prohibited conditions and re-review the actual resulting agreement, or preserve the investigated-and-unsuitable finding. A larger credit amount cannot outweigh the failed independence condition through an opportunity score.

Even if both restrictions are removed, the reviewer still needs eligible demand, actual usable units, rights, expiry, fees and enforceable overage controls. Removal of a veto alone does not establish eligibility, value or acceptance authority.

**Exit condition:** a new exact agreement removes the prohibited clauses and passes its remaining specific review, or the unaccepted offer is declined. No application, acceptance or cancellation is performed by this example.

## Case C: a related-party commercial benefit

A fictional contributor proposes paying a company they own to produce an acknowledgment page. They supplied the quote and would otherwise review the result. Their ownership is a material interest in both selection and payment.

Open a stable arrangement record linked to the quote and existing need. Preserve the private ownership evidence with the actual custodian and prepare only a cleared public relationship summary. The interested contributor can answer factual questions but must not choose the replacement reviewer, approve their own benefit, control the selection or release their own payment.

A qualified disinterested reviewer must assess actual alternatives, necessary scope, price/cost basis, rights and delivery. A second chat agent's draft analysis is not a financial appointment, a governing quorum or an independent legal decision. If no actual independent authority is established, the affected transaction stays **unresolved**.

The financial-control record must separately establish eligible funds, supported maximum exposure, prior approval, actual payable, semantic duplicate search and settlement. A new invoice ID for the same underlying work cannot justify a second payment.

**Exit condition:** actual independent authority and acceptable terms are evidenced and the precisely authorized process is followed, or the transaction is revised/declined. No payee, officer or reviewer is appointed here.

## Case D: changed terms and an uncertain prior attempt

A fictional agreement had a particular version approved by its actual competent authority. Before an attempted renewal, the provider added a new auto-billing term and a requirement for private user data. The attempt returned an ambiguous response. The case does not assume that nothing happened.

Retain the old approval and record the exact new terms, attempted action and uncertainty. The old approval does not automatically cover the changed exposure or data use. The actual operator must reconcile account/contract state through its authorized channel before another attempt.

Do not promise private users' information as a benefit. Identify the affected obligation and lawful response with the actual decision-maker. Possible next steps are an authorized compliant amendment, withdrawal of the unaccepted proposal or a permitted contractual exit. No refund, cancellation, corrective message or change to a live account is authorized by a source-review result.

Retain truthful published findings and the actual record of the attempt. Reconcile any resulting charges or restricted balance through the existing financial controls; do not delete the original event or use a balancing zero.

**Exit condition:** authoritative state and the actual obligation are established, then the permitted next action is decided and its real result recorded. An error response, silence or fresh record ID is not reconciliation.

## Case E: why legal classification and project policy are separate

A fictional sponsor asks for a clearly labeled commercial placement next to a public comparison. The arrangement contains no paid ranking term, but the exact placement, disclosure, costs, rights and reviewer interests have not been evaluated.

The proposed policy does not call the payment a tax-deductible donation or promise a particular tax result. It sends the concrete commercial design to the existing support-model work and competent financial/legal review. The organic comparison must retain its independent inclusion, order and findings; the actual implementation needs review of that behavior.

A platform's ability to publish a paid tier does not approve the project's promise. A potentially acceptable legal category does not waive this draft's independence restrictions. The proposal remains **unresolved**, with no published tier, offer or paid result.

**Exit condition:** the exact independently reviewed model and implementation preserve the stated boundaries and are adopted/authorized for their actual scopes. A hypothetical description alone meets none of those execution conditions.
