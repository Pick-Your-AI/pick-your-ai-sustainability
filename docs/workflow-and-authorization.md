## Two independent tracks

Issue-work status describes delivery: Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, Blocked, Done or Not Pursuing. Opportunity lifecycle describes support: Discovered, Verified, Qualified, Preparing, Submitted, Awarded, Declined, Activated or Reporting.

Done means the issue's researched outcome and review are accepted. An ineligible investigation can be Done while its current disposition is Not Pursuing. Preparing or a draft email does not mean submitted. Awarded does not mean received or usable.

Research readiness remains separate from the application gate. A researcher may examine a 501(c)(3)-dependent program immediately; an application still needs its actual prerequisite evidence.

Related actions may share evidence but require distinct permitted scopes; preparation, negotiation or submission does not imply signing, accepting, activation or spending. Use **Not applicable** only with an action-specific reason. Missing evidence blocks the action that needs it while separately qualified actions retain their result.

## Build and decide a concrete packet

1. **Bind identity and purpose.** Reuse the opportunity ID, provider/program/round and approval-record ID. Link the repository/public-benefit need, actual recipient/contracting party, action and exact artifact revision, including attachments and forms. Keep missing facts Unknown. Do not add the synthetic exercise to a live provider register.
2. **Check the action's evidence needs.** Record current applicable eligibility, intake, terms, deadline and restrictions with source/version/access date and precise claim scope. Separate documentation, vendor assertions, independent findings and estimates. Assess the chosen route separately from its alternatives. Preserve source conflicts and failed access; an approval date is not a successful source-check date.
3. **Bound every commitment.** State benefit type, units or amount/currency, deliverables, term, fees/taxes, renewal, overage, matching funds, exclusivity and exit exposure. Zero cash can still mean nonfinancial obligations. Unknown maxima are not zero; an unverified alert is not an enforceable spending cap. Apply accepted #24 controls to financially consequential actions.
4. **Review independence and disclosure.** Identify sponsor/provider, requester, approver and payee interests; record recusal and an actual independent replacement for an affected review. Do not promise favorable results, competitor exclusion or influence over methodology. Check reporting, branding, publication rights, privacy/data use, portability/export, custody, renewal, overage and termination clauses or explain Not applicable. Applicable unresolved #18/#35 decisions remain visible; this process does not adopt their policies.
5. **Verify authority and decide.** Record the actual approver, capacity, mandate issuer/evidence, permitted actions, limits and validity/revocation conditions. Separate requester, independent reviewer, approver, signer and operator functions under actual entity/host controls. Unfilled roles stay Unassigned; missing required independence blocks the decision. Record Approved, Rejected or Blocked with exact reasons, scope, conditions, date and reviewed version. A review request or conditional proposal is not approval. Reference existing valid authorization instead of asking for a duplicate decision.
6. **Recheck immediately before execution.** Compare actual artifact/version, parties, recipient/account, representations, terms, benefit, cap, dates, qualifications and mandate with the accepted record. Resolve material changes below. Only the actual authorized executor performs the covered action. Preparing this record dispatches nothing.
7. **Retain actual result and obligations.** Record execution time, channel, action/version and a safe receipt reference. Separate attempted, failed, uncertain and confirmed outcomes. An uncertain submission/payment is not proof that nothing happened; reconcile provider/custodian state before retry. Approval alone does not establish Submitted, Awarded or Activated. Link actual reporting, renewal and closeout obligations to the [operating procedure](operations-and-reporting.md), retaining its separate #69 acceptance.

## Validity, amendments and withdrawal

Retain the original decision and add a linked revision when an amendment changes applicant, host, counterparty, recipient/channel, account, artifact/attachments, benefit/amount/currency, financial or nonfinancial commitment, deliverable, material term, privacy/data use, exclusivity, reporting, publication/branding right, deadline, renewal or exit exposure. A new material restriction, conflict, source contradiction, expired condition or changed/revoked mandate also stops reliance on the affected approval.

Identify the changed permitted scope, suspend that action, obtain current evidence and applicable re-review, and record the new decision/version before proceeding. A source recheck confirming identical applicable terms may document continuing validity under the existing approval; the changed review date alone does not require another permission request. Never copy approval to a different provider or silently replace an approved artifact. Record Rejected, Withdrawn, Expired or Superseded without erasing history. Unrelated valid scopes retain their own result.

If discovered after execution, preserve the true execution record and route the unresolved obligation to the actual authorized operator/decision-maker. Do not claim it never happened, cancel a contract, send a corrective message or refund money without authority for that next action.

## Public record and private evidence

Publish only independently cleared fields: stable ID, permitted purpose/action/status, missing gate and safe decision/delivery references. Review permission for each disclosed identity, amount, agreement or statement. Exclude private banking/payment details, donor identities/contacts, personal/tax records, confidential submissions, credentials, sensitive access details and private mandate documents.

Actual evidence belongs in the verified custodian's authorized system with appropriate access/retention. Do not invent that system, appoint its custodian or ask someone to send private documents to a public issue. A permitted public reference can be an opaque safe ID; do not publish access-bearing private links, secret values or hashes of sensitive data. Template prompts describe a real record's requirements, not a place to store private evidence in Git.
