# Benefit activation, reporting and renewal

Owner issue: E09.03. Status: draft process; no award, account, obligation or operating owner is created here.

## Evidence for transitions

| Stage | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Discovered | Distinct provider/program and source lead |
| Verified | Governing current source read, dated program/status/terms assessment and explicit unknowns |
| Qualified | Viable specific use case and applicant route, defensible priority, reviewed restrictions and application gate |
| Preparing | Authorized preparation scope and required evidence checklist |
| Submitted | Actual submission receipt, exact application version and submitting authority |
| Awarded or Declined | Provider decision for that applicant/program, preserving conditions |
| Activated | Actual usable access, funds receipt or accepted delivery, approved operating responsibility and controls |
| Reporting | Actual reporting obligation, deliverables, evidence, due dates and review/receipt |

A qualified finding can still await permission to apply. If a gate fails, retain the historical stage and record the current blocker or Not Pursuing disposition; do not claim a stage transition unsupported by evidence.

## Activation checklist

For every benefit, verify the legal recipient, agreement version, approved authority, scope, expiry, restrictions, measurable use case, operating role, and exit plan.

- Cash: record receipt, currency, restricted purpose, approval and reconciliation references. Private banking detail stays outside GitHub.
- Credits/compute: verify account/model/region eligibility, quotas, rate limits, expiry, overage prevention, permitted benchmarks and data terms; record used capacity and actual avoided cost separately.
- Software: verify project and contributor license scope, renewal, seats, public-repository requirements, allowed use and removal/export.
- Hardware: record donation versus loan, custody, delivery acceptance, shipping, maintenance, data sanitization, insurance/liability questions and return/disposal obligations.
- Services: define the exact review/mentorship/legal/accounting deliverable, competence and confidentiality needs, acceptance and continuity.

Before activation, apply E09.02 authorization and E02.06 financial controls. A repository owner is not automatically a billing owner. A donated resource that does not meet an evidenced need has no assumed avoided cost.

## Reporting and closeout

Create an obligation record from the actual agreement: required output, due date/timezone or trigger, owner, reviewer, source clause, submission evidence and status. Unknown reporting dates stay unknown. Use [the award report](../templates/award-report.md).

Compare planned and delivered outcomes, preserve failed or partial results, reconcile restricted use, and record accepted deviations. Renewals require current eligibility and terms, actual remaining need, authorization and a plan for expiry. At termination, preserve required records, revoke access as appropriate, return loaned assets, settle obligations, and document what remains unresolved.
