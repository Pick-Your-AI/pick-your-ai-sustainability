### 3.1 SFC: membership, stewardship and a negotiated agreement

SFC's application guidance describes a partnership selected on software-freedom and public-benefit fit, licensing and an established community. Software must satisfy both its OSI and DFSG requirements; documentation has separate licensing conditions. It generally excludes projects under a year old or at proof-of-concept stage. Applications are considered on a rolling basis and can take months; no acceptance date or closing deadline for this project is established. The public route is inquiry, application, selection and negotiation, not a grant award. [S1]

Member services include handling suitable donations and contracts and optional asset stewardship. Copyright, trademark or domain assignment is not universally required. The actual rights holder and any chosen transfer still need to be established. [S2]

The six-page **template**, not a PYA contract, gives SFC fund ownership, variance power and board oversight. Agency needs written authority; representation examples are customizable. Exit uses 60 days' notice, a qualified non-private-foundation 501(c)(3) successor and an additional 60-day search if needed. The qualified transfer's 30-day provision remains subject to third-party conditions; absent a successor, disposal follows applicable law. Its fee language uses 10% of gross project revenue, while the application FAQ refers to revenue SFC processes. **Obtain the actual agreed fee base and applicable taxes before acceptance.** [S3; S1 for the FAQ wording]

### 3.2 SPI: an independent associated project with controlled financial services

SPI's HOWTO requires a genuine, substantial and significant free-software project, a board-sponsored application/resolution, and an actual liaison with a succession method. Its illustrative invitation normally allows 60 days for acceptance **after approval**; this is not today's project deadline. There is no association charge, but SPI deducts **5% of the donated amount plus attributable incoming transaction costs**; outgoing transaction costs also use project funds. No PYA acceptance, invitation date or balance is established. [I1]

Association does not make the project part of SPI, change its legal identity or extend SPI's exemption to it. SPI independently assesses requests. A project can leave, but funds may remain until spent or transfer to an eligible US 501(c)(3); ordinary association does not promise indemnification. [I2]

SPI provides documented expense, contract and accounting services, but does not provide fundraising assistance. An SPI officer must sign contracts unless the board specifically authorizes otherwise; a liaison has no general signing power. Assets worth over USD 300 purchased with SPI funds are legally SPI's, and substantial/intangible asset transfers have compatible-charity limits. Exceptional services may need separate project funding. [I3] A historical framework remains linked from the current HOWTO; it is not a newly issued 2026 agreement or evidence of PYA adoption. [I1]

### 3.3 OSC: useful noncharitable infrastructure with actual contract obligations

OSC's public criteria assess legitimacy, activity/use, originality, licensing and appropriate representative standing. Software projects use an organizational repository; shared administration and succession practices matter. Its adjacent-community/event criteria are separate and cannot be assumed from a software repository. The public application offers GitHub verification or manual review. Descriptions of quick processing are not acceptance evidence or a deadline. [O3, O4]

The current fee page says **10% on external incoming funds**, with no host charge for transfers among hosted collectives/funds, no setup/minimum/recurring host charge, and separate processor costs. Actual transaction mix and any discretionary exception are unknown. [O7]

The application-linked agreement's official column controls this reading, not its informal help column. It retains financial oversight, advance written approval for obligations, IP with the project absent a separate transfer, project liability/indemnity and dispute terms. Termination uses 30 days' notice and a 15-day final-expense request; successor, transfer and third-party conditions apply. Three discrepancies need written clarification: its all-deposits fee base versus the operational fee page; 30-day significant-protocol-change notice versus Exhibit A's 10 days; and official effectiveness language versus help text referring to approval. None is silently resolved in the project's favor. [O5, O7]

The closure guide separately addresses eligible successor transfers, outstanding expenses/contracts, restricted-grant holdbacks, employees, cards and recurring funders. Technical/account portability therefore needs a complete inventory and transition plan. [O6] OSC's hosting overview says charitable deductions do not arise from its 501(c)(6) hosting and distinguishes publicly visible transactions from private administrative details. It can administer only funds held with it. [O2]

### 3.4 Independent recognition: lifecycle work is part of the decision

IRS requirements include organizational and operational qualification, exempt purposes, restrictions on private benefit/inurement and political/lobbying activity. A useful open-source product alone does not establish those facts. [L3] The application route is electronic recognition filing, with the appropriate form and its complete eligibility conditions still to be selected. Historical 2020–2022 electronic-filing transition dates on the IRS overview, including its expired 2022 paper grace period, are not current application options or PYA deadlines. [L8]

The 2026 exempt-organization user-fee schedule lists **USD 275 for Form 1023-EZ** and **USD 600 for the other applicable recognition applications in its stated category**. These are scoped published filing charges, not a project budget or a finding that PYA may use the shorter form. Actual selected form, state fees, professional work, registrations, insurance, banking, payroll and recurring administration remain Unknown. Recheck current governing fees and the exact form before payment. [L9]

The IRS lifecycle includes state solicitation questions, annual reporting, employment/unrelated-business matters where applicable, disclosures and significant changes. [L4] Failure to file required returns/notices for three consecutive years can automatically revoke exemption; the actual organization's requirements must first be determined. [L5] Organizing provisions must dedicate assets to exempt/public purposes on dissolution. [L6] Termination has a distinct final-return/notice and state-law process; closing a repository is not that process. [L7] Detailed formation and compliance work stays in [#15](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/15) and [#16](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/16).

## 5. Operating conditions to compare before selecting a path

The following is a decision checklist for actual proposed arrangements. Entries that need private agreements or organizational facts remain unresolved; they do not silently become favorable terms.

| Dimension | Evidence and decision needed |
| --- | --- |
| Restrictions and reporting | Identify permitted work, earmarks, expense evidence, host review, grant deliverables and applicable legal reports. For independence, establish the actual filing calendar. For a host, identify what it performs and what still falls to project representatives. |
| Branding and endorsement | Obtain the exact logo/name/acknowledgment clauses and approved descriptive language. Sponsorship research or a public program page gives no PYA endorsement. No required attribution text is invented. |
| Publication and benchmark rights | Preserve the ability to publish supported methods, limitations, unfavorable results and corrections. Review embargoes, confidentiality, IP licenses and source/provider restrictions against actual deliverables; do not promise rights the project lacks. |
| Privacy and data handling | Determine which ledger/profile fields become public, which administrators see private details, retention, permitted access and publication scope. Keep private applicant, banking, identity, donor and operational material out of public repository evidence. Public summaries can retain safe references and Unknowns. |
| Portability and exit | Inventory funds, restrictions, contracts, data, domains, IP, equipment, accounts and people obligations. Determine permitted successor, approvals, notice, outstanding liabilities and costs before entering an arrangement; use §3's route-specific limits. |
| Renewal and change | Obtain actual agreement duration, change notices, continuing eligibility, verification duties and termination triggers. A rolling intake is not permanent acceptance. No PYA renewal date is established. |
| Overage and recurring cost | A host percentage is not a compute quota. Provider credits, support scope, paid meters and subscriptions have separate limits and expiry. Do not activate a resource without a defensible usage/exit plan under its existing issue. Actual exposure remains Unknown. |
| Independence and conflicts | Separate technical decisions, fund control, legal signature and account administration. Test actual funder publication/ranking rights and related-party arrangements against the purpose assessment and [ethics work #18](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-sustainability/issues/18). |
| Maintenance and succession | Identify real consenting operators for finance, reporting, source/account custody and replacement decisions. Template roles are not appointments. Loss of a maintainer does not automatically eliminate legal obligations. |

The project must choose how much independence it actually needs, identify the administrative capability that solves a real problem, and record any compromise and exit condition. Do not use a lower percentage or a charitable label as a substitute for that analysis.

## 7. Concrete use, value and effort

A useful near-term packet can connect three **proposed** public deliverables to the administrative work each would actually need: an explained comparison lesson using the website's labeled synthetic material; a source/limitations note using the research method; and a reproducible contribution or correction record using the leaderboard. The accepted purpose assessment supplies the dated artifact links and limits. Apple and Android's planning/transfer artifacts remain useful possible access channels, with native execution, distribution and participant outcomes separately evidenced. This brief introduces no new participant protocol, model experiment or release claim.

For each proposed deliverable, record an exact artifact, intended public task, present/proposed status, the needed host or independent-organization service, rights/privacy conditions, a verifiable completion observation and the unresolved evidence. This turns “we need a nonprofit” into a specific service and decision question without assuming that an observed code change is an observed public benefit.

Use the following **valuation method**, with missing inputs left Unknown:

- Hosted cost = verified qualifying receipts for each fee category × the applicable agreed rate, plus processor/FX charges, exceptional services and actual transition/administration cost. Do not count a processor charge twice merely because it appears upstream and in a host statement.
- Independent cost = verified formation/application charges + jurisdiction-specific registrations and professional work + recurring administration/control costs + any future transition or dissolution costs over the same horizon.
- Compare equivalent coverage. A host that performs one task is not the counterfactual price of an entire independent organization. Public fundraising infrastructure is not an award.
- Received cash requires an actual receipt and restrictions; a pledged award, credit face value, possible service and evidenced avoided expense are separate quantities. Avoided cost requires a real alternative purchase or documented effort baseline, not an advertised benefit amount.

No numerical total or comparative score is defensible yet. A preparation estimate can be built from actual numbers of records, agreements and services multiplied by observed review time, with rework and missing evidence separately logged. The existing purpose assessment's proposed effort model remains its own estimate; it is not time already spent, a staff commitment or a quote for this broader decision. **Total project effort, counsel time, recurring capacity, cash required, support received and usable/avoided value are Unknown.** Stop and refine a unit of work when its evidence is missing instead of filling it with an assumed cost or owner.
