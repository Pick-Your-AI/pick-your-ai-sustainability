# Research methodology

Status: initial program method for independent review. This extends the research repository's [method](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_METHOD.md), [workflow](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md) and [source policy](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/main/research/T0-configuration/catalog-data-sources.md), read on 2026-10-19. It does not change their schemas, owners or evidence labels.

## One record per program

Use a stable opportunity ID for one specific provider program. Record program aliases and rounds under that identity unless a new round changes the independently reviewable research outcome. Link the canonical issue and external application URL. Search provider names, program names, aliases, previous rounds and existing organization issues before creating anything.

A landscape task can compare a bounded set of candidates. Promote a candidate into its own issue when its eligibility, restrictions and use case warrant a separate deliverable. Preserve investigated-and-ineligible records.

## Evidence and claims

For each material claim, record the precise source URL, publisher, access timestamp/date, source version or round, access result and applicable jurisdiction, account, product, workload, currency and conditions. Prefer current official program and application terms over historical announcements and marketing summaries. Record a conflict rather than silently choosing the convenient source.

Use separate evidence descriptions:

- Primary documentation records what the publisher says about its program or terms.
- Vendor claims describe uncorroborated capability or performance assertions.
- Independent evidence identifies its method and applicable conditions.
- Estimates show calculation and assumptions.
- Unknown or failed access remains explicit.

The research repository distinguishes source-checked from independently verified claims. Use clear wording here: a program page was read, a reviewer assessed eligibility, an applicant received a provider decision, or support was activated. These are different facts. A fetched page, schema validation or preview fixture proves none of the later stages.

## Required investigation

Determine whether the program exists now, its current application status, the legal applicant requirements, acceptance of an unincorporated project or fiscal sponsor, independent nonprofit conditions, jurisdiction and other restrictions. Record benefit type, actual access/usage limits, expiry, application steps, deadline if confirmed, obligations, public-benefit fit and exact next action.

Evaluate branding, exclusivity, publication and benchmark rights, privacy/data use, portability, renewal, overage, custody and maintenance. Compare existing no-cost capabilities before treating a donation as useful new value. Eligibility for one route never implies eligibility for another.

A known exclusion is Ineligible for that route. Missing facts are Unknown or Not Verified. A feasible unresolved condition is Conditionally Eligible only when its condition and evidence are specified. Never use a blank date, zero cost or optimistic label to hide missing evidence.

## Review and completion

A reviewer reads the governing sources and accepted project evidence, checks the scoring rationale and validates the deliverable against issue criteria. Legal eligibility, significant deadlines, restrictions, valuation and material commitments need a separate qualified review appropriate to the question.

Record the reviewed revision, reviewer identity, scope, decision, limitations and follow-ups. Research can finish with an ineligible recommendation; no application or award follows automatically. Preserve original source dates when a later reviewer only inspected the repository record.
